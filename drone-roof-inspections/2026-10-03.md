I checked 40 name ideas, and most of the .com domains are still available. My top picks are GeorgiaAerialPros.com for the directory and GwinnettAerial.com for your own drone business.

Why two names: a neutral directory name makes the site look fair to the other drone businesses listed on it. Your business gets a local name and is listed like everyone else, with a note on the site that you run it. Neither name uses "roof" or "inspection," since you're launching with real estate, vacation rentals, and builders too.

PeachStateDrones.com: short and memorable.
SoutheastDronePros.com: leaves room to grow, since your 150-mile radius already reaches four other states.

Before you register: check the Georgia Secretary of State business name database and the federal trademark search, and look up the social media handles. Links are in research/name-ideas.md. My pick for your first three:

Water damage restoration. Insurance pays for the job and calls are urgent, so companies pay well for each lead.
Gutters. Easy to rank with plenty of searches.
Junk removal or septic. Junk removal is easier to rank but pays less per job, and septic is the reverse.
Water damage and gutters both get a surge after storms. Along with GeorgiaStormRoof, that gives you a family of storm sites. The same storm brings visitors to all three, and your drone business gets work from them.

One caveat: this score only covers the regular search results. For local searches, Google shows a map with three businesses above them, and that map needs a real business address. A lead site competes for the spots under the map. So before deciding, check the top 10 for "water damage restoration atlanta."

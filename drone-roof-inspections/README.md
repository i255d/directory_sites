# Drone Roof Documentation (Georgia)

Two connected businesses:

1. **Directory site**: a directory of drone roof and property documentation providers in Georgia, organized by county and city. It also brings in leads for my own drone work (home base: Dacula, GA; service radius: 150 miles).
2. **E-book and course**: how to start a drone roof documentation business.

The directory brings in traffic. Homeowners and businesses become leads (for me or other listed providers). Aspiring pilots become e-book and course buyers. Course graduates become new providers who want to be listed.

## Order of work

The directory comes first because Google rankings take 6–12 months to build. The e-book and course come later, using real experience from my own jobs.

1. **Validate:** keyword and competitor research using Similarweb, Ahrefs free tools, and Google Keyword Planner
2. **Get legal:** FAA Part 107 certificate, drone registration, liability insurance, business setup
3. **Build the directory:** Georgia county and city pages, starting with counties closest to Dacula
4. **Start doing paid jobs:** document everything (photos, prices, lessons) for the e-book
5. **Free starter guide:** offered on the directory to collect emails
6. **Full e-book**
7. **Video course:** built from footage of real jobs

## Directory site

**Who visits:**
- Homeowners and property managers who need roof photos after a storm or before a sale
- Roofers, insurance adjusters, and realtors who need drone documentation
- Vacation rental hosts, resorts, builders, and HOAs (other drone services)
- People who want to start a drone business (future e-book and course buyers)

**Ways to make money:**
- Leads for my own jobs (main goal at first)
- Featured listings and claim fees for other providers
- Selling or referring leads outside my area
- Affiliate links for drones, software, insurance, and Part 107 courses

## Important notes

- **Use "documentation" instead of "inspection."** "Inspection" may bring in licensing rules and liability. Georgia's home inspector licensing law (SB 458) starts in 2028. See [research/terminology-and-liability.md](research/terminology-and-liability.md).
- **Capture only, don't judge condition.** I fly and deliver organized footage. Roofers, adjusters, or software judge the condition. See [research/capture-vs-assessment.md](research/capture-vs-assessment.md).
- **Stack services.** Real estate, rentals, resorts, builders, and HOAs fill the time between storms. See [research/service-ideas.md](research/service-ideas.md).
- **Be open about owning the directory** I'm also listed in.

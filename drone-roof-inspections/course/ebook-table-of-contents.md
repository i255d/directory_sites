# E-book: Table of Contents (Draft)

## Working title options

- **Eyes in the Sky: How to Start a Drone Roof Documentation Business**
- **The Drone Roof Documentation Business: Capture the Roof, Not the Liability**
- **Fly, Capture, Get Paid: Building a Drone Property Documentation Business**

**Subtitle idea:** *A practical guide to earning money with a camera drone by documenting roofs and properties for homeowners, roofers, and insurance professionals, without becoming an inspector.*

**Who it's for:** Beginners and hobby drone pilots who want a real business, roofers and realtors adding drones, and side hustlers looking for low startup cost work.

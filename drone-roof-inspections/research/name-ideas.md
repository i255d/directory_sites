# Business and Domain Name Ideas

Checked October 3, 2026 using the official .com registry lookup (Verisign RDAP). Availability can change any day, so register a favorite soon.

## Two names, two jobs

1. **Directory name:** neutral, covers all of Georgia and many services. Sounds like a place to find providers.
2. **My own drone business name:** local, sounds like a service company. Listed in the directory like any other provider, with a disclosure that I run the site.

Keeping them separate makes the directory look fair to other providers, and lets my business stand on its own if I ever sell or change the directory.

Neither name should include "inspection" or "roof" only, since the plan covers roofs plus real estate, rentals, and builders.

### Finder vs. Near Me (checked Oct 3, 2026)

| DronePilotNearMe.com | Taken (2023) |

**GeorgiaDroneFinder.com:** clearly says "find a drone pro here", short, sounds like a directory, no confusion.

**GeorgiaDroneNearMe.com:** follows the "near me" pattern (like dryicenearme.com), but:
- "Drones near me" is often searched by people who **saw a drone overhead** or want **places to fly**, not people hiring a pilot
- "Georgia" + "near me" is a bit redundant
- Google answers "near me" searches using the searcher's location and page content, so having "near me" in the domain helps less than it seems

**Decision lean:** GeorgiaDroneFinder.com as the directory. Optionally buy GeorgiaDroneNearMe.com (~$10/yr) and point it at the directory.

**Pairing options:**
- **Directory GeorgiaDronePilots.com + Business GeorgiaDroneImaging.com:** strongest search match, and the shared "Georgia Drone" is fine if the disclosure is clear
- **Directory GeorgiaAerialPros.com + Business GeorgiaDroneImaging.com:** most separate and neutral

### Thoughts

- **"Documentation" in the name is a strength for my business.** It tells clients what they get and quietly sets expectations (photos, not a condition verdict).
- **It's weaker for the directory.** People don't search "drone documentation"; they search "drone photography", "drone roof inspection", "aerial photos". A directory name with "aerial" or "drone" plus "pros" or "finder" fits search better.
- **Long names are harder** to say on the phone, fit on a truck or shirt, and type correctly. Under ~20 letters is ideal, but a clear long name is better than a short confusing one.

### Possible pairing

- **Directory:** GeorgiaAerialPros.com
- **My business:** GeorgiaAerialDocumentation.com (plus GeorgiaArialDocumentation.com as a redirect)

## Before registering

- [ ] Search the [Georgia Secretary of State business name database](https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov/BusinessSearch) for the business name
- [ ] Search [USPTO trademarks](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) for conflicts
- [ ] Google the name to make sure no local company uses something similar
- [ ] Check social handles (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok)
- [ ] Register the domain (about $10–$20/year), and consider grabbing close variations

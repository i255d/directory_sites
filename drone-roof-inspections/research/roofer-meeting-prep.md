# Roofer Meeting Prep: Lead Generation

## What I want to walk away knowing

1. How this roofer gets customers today, and what it costs
2. What a good lead is worth to him
3. Whether he'd pay me for leads, how, and how much
4. Whether he'd use drone documentation (from me) in his sales process
5. Whether he'd introduce me to other roofers

This is mostly a **listening meeting**. The goal is information and a possible small trial, not a big commitment.

## Questions to ask

### How he gets work now
- Where do most of your jobs come from? (referrals, Google, door knocking, storm canvassing, insurance, Angi/HomeAdvisor, Google Local Services Ads, Facebook, yard signs)
- Which source brings the best customers? Which is the worst?
- Do you pay for leads now? Who from, and what does each lead cost?
- What do you spend on marketing in a typical month? After a storm?

### What a lead is worth
- What's your average job size? Repair vs. full replacement?
- What share of your jobs are insurance claims?
- Out of 10 leads, how many turn into a signed job?
- How fast do you need to contact a lead before it goes cold?
- What makes a lead bad? (wrong area, renter, just shopping, already signed with someone)

### Capacity and area
- How many more jobs a month could you handle?
- What counties and cities do you serve? Where do you want more work?
- What happens to your workload after a big hail storm?
- Slow seasons?

### Paying for leads
- Would you pay per lead, per booked appointment, or a monthly fee for a territory?
- What would you pay per lead for: a roof repair? a replacement? a storm damage job?
- Do you want leads exclusive to you, or are shared leads OK at a lower price?
- How do you want leads delivered? (text, email, phone call, CRM)
- What would make you stop paying? (quality, volume, speed)

### Drone documentation
- Do you or your crew climb every roof now? Any interest in drone photos instead?
- Would aerial photos help you quote faster, sell more jobs, or deal with insurance adjusters?
- What would you pay per roof for an organized photo package?
- Do you already use EagleView, Hover, or similar for measurements?

### Others
- Do you know other roofers who'd want leads in areas you don't cover?
- Would you be open to a small trial?

## What to bring

- A short one-page overview: who I am, the Georgia directory plan, drone documentation, lead generation idea
- A sample lead form (what info a lead would include: name, phone, address, roof issue, storm date, insurance claim yes/no, photos)
- Notes on what I've learned (hail data for his counties, if known in advance)
- A notebook to write down his numbers

## A trial I could propose

Keep it small and low risk for both sides:

- **Option 1: Ad-funded test.** He funds $300–$500 in Google Ads for roof/storm searches in his area, I build a simple landing page and route leads to him. We both learn real cost per lead.
- **Option 2: Pay per lead trial.** 10 leads at an introductory price, with clear rules for what counts as a valid lead.
- **Option 3: Drone documentation trial.** I capture 3–5 roofs for him (once I have my Part 107 certificate and insurance) at a discount, in exchange for honest feedback and a testimonial.

## What NOT to promise

- **No promised lead volumes or timelines** before the site ranks. SEO takes months.
- **No claims about roof condition or insurance outcomes** in any lead or drone work.
- **No exclusive territory for free.** If he wants exclusivity, that's a paid arrangement.
- **No paid drone work** until I have my FAA Part 107 certificate and insurance.

## Things to check before any deal

- Georgia rules on contractor solicitation, storm canvassing, and insurance-related advertising (the roofer is responsible for his licensing, but lead wording matters)
- Lead-related laws: getting consent from homeowners to share their info and be contacted (TCPA rules for calls and texts)
- A simple written agreement: price, what counts as a valid lead, payment terms, how to dispute bad leads, how to end the arrangement

## Good signs from this meeting

- He already pays for leads and can name the cost per lead and close rate
- He wants more volume, especially in specific counties
- He gives a real price he'd pay per lead or per appointment
- He's interested in drone photos for quoting or insurance work
- He offers to introduce me to other roofers

## Warning signs

- Only wants free leads, or "pay only if the job closes" with no limits
- Unclear or unwilling to share numbers
- Wants exclusive rights to large areas without paying
- Complaints about every lead source (may be hard to satisfy)

# Drone Service Ideas Beyond Roofs

Roof documentation is the starting point, but the same drone, skills, and customers open up many related services. These could become extra categories in the directory, extra income for my own business, and extra modules in the course.

Ratings: **Demand** = how often people pay for it. **Liability** = risk level (marketing footage is low; anything that sounds like judging a condition is higher).

## Real estate and rentals

| Service | Customers | Demand | Liability | Notes |
|---------|-----------|--------|-----------|-------|
| Listing photos and video | Realtors, home sellers, FSBO sellers | High | Low | Most common paid drone job. Repeat work from realtors. |
| Land and lot listings | Land sellers, realtors, developers | High | Low | Drones show boundaries, acreage, access. Rural GA has lots of land sales. |
| Short-term rental marketing | VRBO/Airbnb hosts, property managers | Medium | Low | House plus the area: lake, mountains, downtown. North GA mountains (Blue Ridge, Helen, Ellijay) and Lake Lanier are strong markets near Dacula. |

| 3D tours and maps of property | Realtors, luxury listings | Low–Med | Low | Add-on to listing packages. |

## How this affects the project

- **Directory scope:** Consider naming and structuring the site around **drone property services in Georgia**, with roof documentation as the lead category. Other categories (real estate, rentals, construction, land) can be added as the site grows.
- **My own business:** Real estate, rental, and builder work is low-risk and fills the schedule between storms. Roof documentation brings the big spikes after hail.
- **Recurring revenue ideas:** Annual pre-storm baselines, monthly construction progress, HOA contracts, realtor packages.
- **Course:** Add a module on "stacking services" so students aren't dependent on storms alone.
- **Liability:** Marketing footage is low risk. Anything that sounds like judging condition (roofs, solar, chimneys) should use documentation wording. Mapping should avoid the word "survey" because land surveying is licensed in Georgia.

- **Business:** Georgia Drone Pros LLC, founded 2016, 1 employee, Warner Robins with an office in Byron
- **Real site:** [georgiadronepros.hd.pics](https://georgiadronepros.hd.pics/). Both .com domains forward there. "hd.pics" is a real estate photography delivery platform.
- **What it is:** a gallery of real estate listings photographed for local realtors (Warner Robins, Perry, Kathleen, Cordele, Byron, Fort Valley)
- **Marketing:** [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/georgiadronepros) (515 followers), posts each new listing; also listed on [CubiCasa](https://www.cubi.casa/photographer/georgia-drone-pros/)
- **Prices** ([order form](https://georgiadronepros.hd.pics/order)):
  - Photography + 2D floor plan (1,000–5,000 sq ft): **$280**
  - Gold package (photos + FPV drone fly-through + floor plan + marketing): **$460**
  - Platinum marketing package: **$520+**
  - Home + land package with property lines on aerials (up to 150 acres): **$675**
  - FPV drone fly-through add-on: **$150**
- **Takeaways:**
  - A one-person drone business can run on **realtor repeat work**. The gallery shows dozens of realtors using them again and again.
  - Their site is a gallery, not an SEO site, so it likely gets little Google traffic. Their work comes from realtor relationships.
  - **Real pricing data** for the e-book (Chapter 12) and for my own pricing.
  - Mostly **outside my main area** (Warner Robins is ~97 miles from Dacula), so not a direct competitor for me.

## How to check traffic yourself (free)

1. **Similarweb:** go to [similarweb.com/website](https://www.similarweb.com/website/), type the domain, and click Analyze. Free account allows about 15 lookups per day. Very small sites may show "not enough data", which itself means traffic is low (likely under ~5,000 visits/month).
2. **Ahrefs Traffic Checker:** go to [ahrefs.com/traffic-checker](https://ahrefs.com/traffic-checker), enter the domain. Shows estimated Google search traffic and top keywords.
3. For Georgia Drone Pros, check **georgiadronepros.hd.pics** too, since that's where the real site lives.

## What's missing (my opportunity)

Existing material is mostly **general** ("start any drone business") or **technical** (how to fly and capture a roof). I found nothing that combines:

1. **Roof and property documentation as a business**, not just as a flying skill
2. **"Documentation, not inspection"** wording and staying out of licensed territory
3. **Capture-only models**: working for roofers, adjusters, and pilot networks without judging condition
4. **Storm response**: using hail data to know where and when to market
5. **Stacking services** (real estate, rentals, builders) to fill time between storms
6. **State-specific rules**, e.g. Georgia's new home inspector licensing law
7. **Getting leads from a directory**, using the directory I'm building as a real example

## Pricing guide for my course

- Free e-books and lead magnets are common, so a **free guide** works well to collect emails.
- Business courses sell for **$149–$350** one-time.
- Memberships run around **$97/month** (Drone U).
- Full programs go up to **$1,595** (Drone Launch Academy).
- Niche roof courses sell as cheap as **$57**, so a roof course needs clear business value to charge more.

Possible structure:
- **Free:** "Drone Roof Documentation: Starter Guide" e-book (email signup)
- **Paid e-book:** $19–$29 on Amazon and my site
- **Course:** $197–$297
- **Upsell:** templates (contracts, disclaimers, roofer outreach emails), a directory listing, or coaching

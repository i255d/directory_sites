Researched October 3, 2026 from notes in `README.md`. Autocomplete in `autocomplete.csv`.

## The idea

Start with Halal restaurants. Later, broaden or spin off Kosher and Keto (personal interest + a kosher-observant former partner).

These are **ad / featured-listing directories**, not $50–$150 lead sites. A diner spends $15–$40. A restaurant will not pay water-damage prices for a seat.

## Demand (Google autocomplete)

Real, local, and already showing **Gwinnett**:
- *halal restaurants near me / atlanta / lawrenceville ga / alpharetta / open now / pakistani*

- *halal grocery / meat near me* (Lawrenceville again)
- *kosher restaurants near me / atlanta / dairy / airport*

People search this. Google also answers it with **Maps**, which is the main competitor.

## Halal vs Kosher vs Keto: keep them separate

| | Halal | Kosher | Keto |
|---|---|---|---|
| What the searcher needs | "Is the meat actually halal? Alcohol? Certified?" | "Is it certified (OU, etc.)? Meat or dairy?" | "Does this menu have low-carb options?" |
| Audience | Muslims + some travelers | Observant Jews (smaller) | Dieters (not a faith rule) |
| Trust problem | Yes. "Halal-friendly" vs certified vs hand-slaughtered | Yes, and stricter | No. Any restaurant can have a salad. |
| Same site? | **No.** Mixing them looks untrustworthy to the people who care most. | Separate site if it works. | Better as a **filter on a city food site**, or skip. Keto searches also pull meal-prep and delivery. |

One "special diets" site would be weaker than three focused sites, and weaker than Maps.

## Money

- **Ads:** possible if you reach 30K+ visits/mo. Hard while Zabihah and Maps exist.
- **Featured listings:** restaurants, grocers, butchers. Maybe $50–$200/mo in a city. Atlanta may only have dozens of true Halal spots, not thousands.
- **Leads:** weak. A table is not a $5,000 foundation job.

To hit $1K–$5K from ads alone you still need roughly **30K–150K visits/mo**. A Georgia-only Halal site is unlikely to get there. A national one means competing with Zabihah.

## Georgia angle

Autocomplete already names **Lawrenceville, Alpharetta, Buford Highway, Duluth, Johns Creek**. That is your backyard. A **Georgia Halal guide** (restaurants + grocers + meat markets, with who certifies the meat and whether they serve alcohol) could be useful locally.

`halalgeorgia.com` looks available. `georgiahalal.com` was taken Feb 2025. `halalatlanta.com` and `atlantahalal.com` are taken.

Local and trusted is the only opening. A thin national scrape is not.

## Clean keto, dirty keto, and dining out (Oct 3, 2026)

Autocomplete in `keto-autocomplete.csv`.

### Clean vs dirty keto

Both stay low-carb enough to keep you in ketosis. The difference is **food quality**, not the carb count.

| | Clean keto | Dirty keto |
|---|---|---|
| Rule | Whole foods + low carb | Hit the macros any way |
| Protein | Meat, fish, eggs (better if grass-fed / wild, but any unbreaded meat works) | Same, plus processed meats, fast-food patties |
| Fat | Butter, olive oil, avocado, tallow, coconut oil | Seed oils (soybean, canola, "vegetable oil"), processed cheese, keto bars |
| Carbs | Non-starchy vegetables, maybe berries | Diet soda, sugar-free syrups, bunless fast food, "keto" packaged snacks |
| Typical restaurant order | Grilled steak, steamed broccoli, olive oil or butter, salad, no bun | Bunless burger, American cheese, bacon, sugar-free ketchup, fries skipped |

Both can work for weight loss. Clean keto is what people mean when they care about energy, inflammation, and not living on processed food. Dirty keto is what most restaurant keto actually is, because kitchens cook in seed oil and thicken sauces with sugar or cornstarch.

### Can you eat clean keto at a restaurant?

**Yes, if you order simply and ask questions.** You will not get farm-perfect clean keto at Chili's. You can get **clean enough**:
- Steakhouse, Brazilian steakhouse, Greek, or a place that grills meat and will do vegetables in butter
- Skip bread, rice, pasta, potatoes, tortillas, sweet glazes
- Sauce on the side
- Ask them to cook in butter or olive oil, not "vegetable oil"

You cannot reliably get clean keto at most Chinese, Thai, or fast-casual places. Hidden carbs (sugar in sauces, cornstarch, breading) and seed oils are the default. That meal is dirty keto at best.

Google already suggests *clean keto restaurants lawrenceville ga / dacula ga / buford / duluth / snellville* — people near you are searching this.

### Questions to ask (print this / put on the site)

Ask the server, or call ahead. You want yes/no answers, not a debate.

**Must ask (hidden carbs)**
1. Is this breaded, glazed, or dusted with flour or cornstarch?
2. Does the marinade or sauce have sugar, honey, or sweet chili?
3. Can I get the sauce / dressing on the side? What's in the house dressing?
4. Can you swap the starch (rice, potato, pasta, bun) for extra vegetables or a side salad?

**Must ask (clean vs dirty fats)**
5. What oil do you cook the meat and vegetables in? (Want butter, olive oil, tallow, or avocado oil. "Vegetable oil" = dirty keto.)
6. Can the kitchen cook mine in butter or olive oil?

**Nice to know**
7. Is the meat grass-fed / the fish wild-caught? (Often no. Eat it anyway if the rest is clean.)
8. Do you have a bunless / lettuce-wrap option?
9. Any broth or steak that's just meat, salt, and fat? (Good for carnivore too.)

If they don't know the oil or the sauce, treat it as dirty keto and decide if that's acceptable tonight.

### Is there enough traffic? Keto vs carnivore

**Keto dining is a "how to order" topic, not a restaurant directory.** Autocomplete is full of:
- *how to eat keto at a mexican / chinese / italian / thai / sushi / hibachi restaurant*
- *keto dining out*, *keto eating out chart*, *keto fast food*
- *clean keto vs dirty keto*
- *keto restaurants near me* (Google will show Maps + a few listicles)

People do not need a list of "keto restaurants." Almost any restaurant can do a grilled protein + vegetables. They need **rules, cuisine-by-cuisine orders, and the question list above**.

**Carnivore ("only meat") is smaller and already has a directory:** [carnivoremap.com](https://www.carnivoremap.com/) (793+ restaurants, ratings for meat quality, organs, seed oils). Autocomplete exists (*carnivore restaurants near me*, *carnivore diet restaurant hacks*) but many results are steakhouse *names* ("Carnivore Steakhouse"), not the diet.

| Play | Traffic type | Site type | Odds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clean vs dirty keto + dining-out guides | Evergreen explainers | Blog / reference, not a map | Possible ads; needs Keyword Planner |
| "How to eat keto at [cuisine]" | Many similar phrases | Programmatic pages (like towing-by-model) | Crowded with Healthline-style sites |
| Keto restaurant directory | "Near me" | Map of restaurants | Weak. Maps already does this. |
| Carnivore restaurant map | Smaller | Directory | Taken (Carnivore Map). |

**Guess without Keyword Planner:** keto *information* searches are real; keto *directory* searches are not a $1K–$5K business. Carnivore is a tighter tribe with higher passion and lower volume.

## Tools for traffic and keyword difficulty
**We have access to: Ahrefs and Similarweb.** Use these first.

- Ahrefs Site Explorer: organic traffic, top pages, and ranking keywords for each competitor domain.
- Ahrefs Keywords Explorer: volume and keyword difficulty (KD) for the seed list. Set the country to US and check the "Clicks" and "Traffic potential" columns.
- Ahrefs Content Gap: put 3 to 5 competitor domains in to see keywords they rank for that his site doesn't.
- Similarweb: total visits and traffic sources for competitors and directories (eHardhat, Angi, etc.). Small local sites may show no data here, so use Ahrefs for those.

Other options, if needed:
- Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (free, own site only)
- Ahrefs free checkers (traffic and top pages for any domain, limited keyword difficulty)
- Semrush free account (about 10 searches a day): Domain Overview, Keyword Magic Tool
- Ubersuggest (a few free searches a day)
- Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account; volume only, not true difficulty)
- Google Search Console (free; what his site already ranks for)
- Local Falcon or BrightLocal (Google Maps pack rankings)

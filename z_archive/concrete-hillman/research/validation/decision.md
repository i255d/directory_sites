# Decision (preliminary): IP Concrete site vs. neutral directory

Status: preliminary, Oct 4, 2026. Spec: [../validation-spec.md](../validation-spec.md). Evidence: [findings.md](findings.md).

## Scorecard (1 = bad, 5 = great)

| Factor | Score | Evidence |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Search demand (group A) | 3 | Minnesota town and metro phrases with data add up to about 1,100 searches a month (Ahrefs, a floor). Most small towns have under 10. "Concrete contractors near me" is 33,000 in the US, but that goes to the Maps results. |
| Value per lead | 4 | Jobs are $5,000 or more. Cost per click is only $3 to $6. |
| Rankability | 4 | Competitor sites checked have DR 0 to 7 and no Google traffic. Nobody covers Pierz, Hillman, Royalton well. Maps results still matter most. |
| Competitor weakness | 4 | Erickson, the strongest, gets about 90 visits a month on concrete topics (most of its 1,500 is asphalt). Three other sites got 0. |
| Season and trend | 3 | Searches growing year over year, but the winter drop in Minnesota is large. |
| Buyer in hand and capacity | 3 | Brother is the buyer (2 to 4 jobs a week at $5,000+). Capacity and Google profile unknown. |
| Income model | 3 | Rough estimate: about 1.5 extra jobs a month, around $7,500 of work in season. Profit per job unknown. |
| Repeatable | 3 | Not tested (Step 11). |
| Fit | 4 | Family business, direct contact. Unknown: masonry services. |
| **Total** | **31 / 45** | |

Guide from the spec for a 9-factor scorecard: 38 to 45 Go, 28 to 37 Pivot (narrower or different angle), under 28 No-go.

## Preliminary call: Pivot
Do not build a neutral directory first. Build a **site for IP Concrete** (one page per town he serves, cost pages, how-to pages, photos, reviews) together with a **Google Business Profile**. Add other contractors later only if the site earns traffic and he has more leads than he can handle.

## Comparison with the water damage project
Roughly the same total (about 31 by my rough scoring of the water notes). Concrete has a buyer you can talk to and weaker competitors. Water damage has higher demand and a $123 top ad bid but no buyer yet.

## What would change this
- He is booked solid and turns work away: lower buyer and income scores.
- He has room for 1 to 2 more jobs a week and no Google profile: raise both.
- Masonry keywords show real demand (list 3 not run yet): raise demand.
- Ahrefs SERP numbers for Premium Concrete, Heb's and DRSC differ from the zeros so far.

# Interviews (Step 8)

## Brother (owner, Hillman MN) - Oct 4, 2026

What I know so far (from me, not yet from a sit-down):
- Does **2 to 4 jobs a week, each over $5,000**.

Rough math (assumptions, to confirm):
- 2 to 4 jobs x $5,000 = **$10,000 to $20,000+ a week** in sales.
- If the season is about 30 weeks (roughly April to November): **$300,000 to $600,000+ a year**.
- One extra $5,000 job a month adds about $60,000 a year in sales. Profit per job is unknown.

Questions still to ask:
1. How does he get jobs today? (word of mouth, Facebook, signs, builders, repeat customers)
2. Is he booked out? How many weeks of backlog? Could he take 1 or 2 more jobs a week?
3. What is the average job, and what is the profit on one? What types of jobs are best (driveways, pole barn slabs, foundations, patios)?
4. Which towns does he work in, and how far will he drive?
5. How many crews? What does he turn down?
6. Is he on Google Maps (Google Business Profile)? Any reviews?
7. Would he pay for leads, and how much per lead or per job?
8. Would he refer overflow jobs to other contractors for a fee?
9. Does he want his name on the site, or a neutral directory brand?

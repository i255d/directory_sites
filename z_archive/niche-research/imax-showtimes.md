# IMAX theaters + seat availability: First Look

Noted October 3, 2026. Trigger: a new IMAX title (~3 months ago, *The Odyssey*) sold out except late shows. Finding other IMAX screens and leftover seats took too much clicking.

## The job to be done

One page: every IMAX near Dacula / Gwinnett / Atlanta, tonight’s times, seats left. That is what you wanted.

## Demand

Autocomplete is real and spikes with the title:
- *imax near me*, *imax lawrenceville ga*, *imax showtimes*, *imax tickets available in atlanta*
- *the odyssey* attached to IMAX searches

Between blockbusters, volume drops. This is **event traffic**, not a steady 30K visits.

## Who already owns the useful part

| What you needed | Who has it |
|---|---|
| List of IMAX screens | [imax.com](https://www.imax.com/) theater finder, Google Maps |
| Showtimes | AMC, Regal, Cinemark apps |
| Seats left | Fandango, Atom, the chain — **live inventory** |

Metro Atlanta only has a handful of true IMAX screens (e.g. AMC Lawrenceville, AMC Forsyth, Regal Atlantic Station, Fernbank, plus a few others). A directory of 8 buildings is a bookmark, not a business.

## Why we should not build the availability site

Seat maps are the chains’ product. They do not publish a friendly “compare all IMAX seats” feed you can reuse. Scraping ticket checkout pages is against their terms and breaks constantly. Without live seats you only have a list of theaters with outbound links — the same digging you already did, just on your domain.

Fandango and Google already do “showtimes near me.” You would be an affiliate middle page.

## Verdict

The frustration is valid. The site is not a $1K–$5K directory. Too few locations, traffic only when a giant IMAX movie hits, and the one feature that would make it worth using (seats left) is locked.

Keep it as a personal bookmark list if you want. Do not make it a product.

# Finding a Repeatable $1K–$5K/Month Site

Started October 3, 2026.

## The goal

One site that earns **$1,000–$5,000 a month**, then copy the same model to the next niche or city.

## What today's research taught us

| | Towing capacity (national reference) | Roofer leads (local) |
|---|---|---|
| Value per visitor | ~$0.02 (ads) | $10–$100 (what roofers pay Google per click) |
| Visitors needed for $3K/mo | ~150,000 pageviews | ~1,000–3,000 |
| Time to get there | Years | Months |

**Value per visitor matters more than search volume.** National reference sites need huge traffic. Local service sites in expensive trades need very little.

## The repeatable model: local lead site

A site for one **expensive home or property service** in one metro (or state). It ranks in Google, collects calls and form requests, and sends them to a local business that pays:
- **Flat monthly rent** for exclusive leads: typically $500–$2,000/mo per site, or
- **Per lead:** typically $30–$150 depending on job size

The same template copies to: same niche in a new city, or a new niche in the same city.

### Math for $1K–$5K/mo
- 20–50 leads a month at $50–$100 each
- At 2–4% of visitors becoming a lead: **~1,000–2,500 visitors a month**
- That's within reach for a local site in a weak niche, unlike 150K pageviews for towing

## How to score a niche

| Factor | Good sign |
|---|---|
| Job size | $2,000+ per job (the business can afford $50–$150 a lead) |
| Click price (Keyword Planner, Georgia) | $10+ top-of-page bid |
| Search volume (Georgia) | "near me" + city phrases at 1K+ a month combined |
| Competition | Local results show small business sites, not only Angi/HomeAdvisor/Yelp |
| Buyers | Many local businesses doing it, so someone will rent the site |
| Repeatable | Same demand exists in other Georgia cities and other states |
| Fits you | Bonus if storm-related (feeds GeorgiaDroneImaging) or drone-related |

## Water damage and gutters: deeper look (Oct 3, 2026)

### Who ranks (web search, "water damage restoration atlanta" / "gutter installation atlanta ga")

**Water damage:** Roto-Rooter (national), Atlanta Restoration (local, Johns Creek), SERVPRO (franchise), **Water Pro Inc. (based in Dacula)**, irs-247.com (multi-city restoration site, looks like a lead-gen setup).
**Gutters:** **Best Pick Reports holds 3 of the top 5** (paid directory of "Best Pick" companies), Peach State Installations, Top Gutter ATL (Gmail address, small local).

- Best Pick Reports proves a **paid local directory already works for Atlanta gutters**. Its model: invite-only listing fee, plus a fee per call/web lead or a percent of the job.
- Water Pro Inc. in Dacula is a **possible first buyer** to talk to (local to you).

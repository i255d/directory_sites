**What that tells us:** 30K–85K visits a month from **ads alone ≈ $1,000–$2,000/mo**. Add featured listings or leads in a niche where businesses have money and it's more.

Frey Chu's niche rules:
- "Near me" searches with **~40K+ monthly volume** and low difficulty
- A "bounty": an existing directory that's **outdated or clunky** and still ranks
- Boring, unsexy niches beat exciting ones
- Example searches he cites: *splash pads near me* (32K, KD 0), *plasma donation near me* (40K+), *laser tag near me*, *basketball courts near me*

## Hands-on review (Oct 3, 2026)

- **freecampsites.net:** search by state doesn't work
- **allstays.com:** "Get started" goes to a paywall
- **pickyourown.org:** dated, lots of outbound links, some state links broken, ad popups so big the site is nearly unusable
- **atlantatrails.com** (not atlantatrail.com): looks very nice. Two-person Georgia hiking guide, not a scraped directory. Full notes below.

Broken, clunky sites that still get traffic are the "bounty" signal: the demand is proven, and the current answer is bad.

### Seasonality
- One-season niches (pumpkin patches Sep–Oct, Christmas trees Nov–Dec, splash pads Jun–Aug) earn most of the year's money in 2–3 months.
- **Fix 1: stack seasons on one site.** Pick-your-own across the year: strawberries (Apr–May) → blueberries/peaches (Jun–Jul) → apples (Aug–Oct) → pumpkins (Sep–Oct) → Christmas trees (Nov–Dec). pickyourown.org's owner split these across three sites; one site could carry all of them.
- **Fix 2: choose year-round niches:** thrift stores, plasma centers, notaries, RV dump stations, truck stops, basketball courts, halal restaurants.
- Check: Similarweb's monthly chart for pickyourown.org and pumpkinpatchesandmore.org shows how big the swings are.

## Atlanta Trails (looked at Oct 3, 2026)

**Site:** [atlantatrails.com](https://www.atlantatrails.com/) (registered Sep 28, 2009). `atlantatrail.com` is empty and available.

**What it is:** a **hand-written Georgia hiking and running guide**, not a data directory. Two people: Eric Champlin (ex-ad creative director) and Rob Sollie (ex-landscape architect), as Summit19 Studio LLC in Hiawassee. They also run Asheville Trails and **Trailful Outdoor Co.**, a physical hiking shop.

**Size:** ~252 sitemap URLs; ~195 posts; ~140 trail guides. Last post update Sep 7, 2026. WordPress + Yoast.

**A typical page** (High Shoals Falls): original photos, mile-by-mile hike writeup, dog-friendly note, parking, GPS, elevation profile, map, pack list, nearby hikes, and a pitch to visit their shop. They hiked every trail themselves.

**How they make money:** display ads, affiliate/gear mentions, the brick-and-mortar shop, and a second city site (Asheville). They say they have helped 8 million hikers find 30 million adventures since 2009. In 2015 the founder said they were tracking **5 million pageviews a year**. Third-party traffic estimates today are all over the place (3K–250K+ visitors/month), so check Similarweb.

**Can you copy this?** The *look* and the *local Georgia outdoor* idea, yes. The *business*, no, not as a weekend data site. This took 17 years of hiking, photography, and writing. Beating it would mean becoming outdoor writers, not building a spreadsheet.

**What to take from it**
- A **beautiful local guide** still works in Georgia, and people notice the difference from pickyourown.org.
- They win on **original photos + first-hand details** (parking, dogs, GPS, elevation), the same extras that would make a basketball-court or trail directory useful.
- They copied the site to a second city (Asheville). That's the "do it over and over" part, but only after the first city is real.
- This is a **passion + retail** business, not a $1K–$5K passive directory.

**Similarweb next:** atlantatrails.com, and if it shows well, ashevilletrails.com.

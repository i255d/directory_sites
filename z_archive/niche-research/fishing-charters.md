# Fishing charter directory: First Look

October 3, 2026. Autocomplete in `fishing-charter-autocomplete.csv`.

## Demand

Yes at **destination towns**. Autocomplete is packed:
- *fishing charters near me* + prices, cheap, today, kid friendly
- Towns: Destin, Panama City Beach, Key West, Cape Coral, Savannah, Tybee, Orange Beach, Myrtle Beach
- *inshore* / *offshore* + those same towns
- *lake lanier fishing charters* + striper, prices
- *fishing charter prices* / cost per person

This is vacation + “book a $500–$1,000 boat,” not a 2-cent ad click.

We do **not** have Keyword Planner volumes yet. List: `fishing-charter-keyword-planner-list.txt` (run US).

## Who already owns it

| Site | Model |
|---|---|
| **FishingBooker** | The big one. Book online, take a cut. Worldwide. |
| Captain Experiences | Same idea |
| saltwaterpros.com | Verified captains, many states |
| charterfishingboat.com | 1,000+ Florida listings, call the captain (no booking fee) |
| Find the Fishing | Guide search |

A new national “all charters” list is FishingBooker with worse booking. Do not start there.

## Money (if you rank in a town)

Half day inshore ~$500–$650; full day ~$1,000+; offshore more. Captains pay for:
- **Booked trips** (affiliate / marketplace cut), or
- **Leads** ($50–$150 is plausible; a sold trip is worth hundreds to them)

A **Cape Coral** or **Destin** page that sends 10 booked trips a month can beat a 30K-visit hoop site on ads.

## Enough traffic for a directory?

| Scope | Traffic | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| National “charters near me” | High | Low. FishingBooker + Google Maps |
| One famous town (Destin, Key West, PCB) | High, seasonal | Crowded |
| **Cape Coral / SWFL** (you go there) | Medium, you have notes | Best fit: add to the vacation guide |
| **Lake Lanier + GA coast** (Savannah, Brunswick, Tybee) | Smaller, closer to home | Second town set |
| Gwinnett-only | Almost none | Skip |

**Verdict:** there is enough traffic **per fishing town**, and the visitor is worth much more than a basketball-court visitor. There is not a hole for another US-wide charter marketplace.

Do it as **pages on the Cape Coral site** (and later Lanier / Savannah), not as a third standalone brand — unless Keyword Planner shows huge town volumes and Ahrefs shows a weak local list ranking.

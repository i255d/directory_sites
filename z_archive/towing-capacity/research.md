# Towing Capacity Site: First Look

Researched October 3, 2026. Autocomplete results in `data/autocomplete.csv`.

## What kind of site this is

Not a local directory. It's a **national reference site**: one page per year/make/model (and trim), plus guides (state towing laws, trailer speed limits, trailer tire ratings, GCWR/GVWR explained) and calculators. Income comes from **display ads, affiliate links, and advertisers**, not local listings.

## Demand (Google autocomplete)

Every vehicle tested has a full set of suggestions, with year, "chart", "by year" and "by VIN" variants:
- *tacoma towing capacity 2026 / 2025 / chart / by year*, *2024 tacoma towing capacity sr5*
- *f150 / silverado / ram 1500 towing capacity by vin / chart by year*
- *highlander, rav4, explorer towing capacity [year]*: SUVs and crossovers too
- Tools: *towing capacity calculator (by vin)*, *how much can my car tow (by vin)*, *tow rating vin lookup*
- Laws and speed: *trailer speed limits by state*, *trailer speed limit california*, *towing laws by state*, *trailer brake requirements by state (map)*, *trailer tire speed rating chart*
- Buying: *best truck for towing camper / travel trailer / 10,000 lbs*, *weight distribution hitch*

## What this means

**Good**
- Demand is big, national, and evergreen (new model year every year = new pages every year).
- Google is ranking **brand-new sites** right now (TrimFacts at 12 days old, TowingIQ at 7 months).
- Data is available: every manufacturer publishes a free annual towing guide (PDF).
- Competitors **disagree with each other** (e.g., Georgia's towing speed: rigcalc says 65 mph, RV Nerds says 70). Accurate, sourced data can stand out.

**Bad / risks**
- **Gold rush.** At least five towing databases launched in 2026, probably AI-assisted. More are coming. Google updates often wipe out batches of similar sites.
- **Google answers the question itself.** For "tacoma towing capacity," Google's AI answer often shows "6,500 lbs" at the top, so many searchers never click. Simple max-number pages will get fewer visits than search volume suggests. Detailed trim/config charts and calculators are what earn clicks.
- **Big established players:** Car and Driver, Kelley Blue Book, Edmunds, dealers, manufacturers.
- **Accuracy liability:** a wrong number can contribute to an accident. Needs sources on every page and clear disclaimers ("check your owner's manual and door sticker").

## Who might pay (advertisers and affiliates)

- **Affiliate:** etrailer.com (hitches, brake controllers, wiring), Amazon, weight-distribution hitch brands, RV rental (Outdoorsy, RVshare), trailer and RV insurance
- **Advertisers / leads:** RV dealers, trailer dealers, truck dealers, hitch installers, boat dealers, moving and equipment rental (U-Haul, Home Depot truck rental)
- **Display ads:** auto/RV content typically earns more per visitor than general content

## Search volume: Keyword Planner, United States (Oct 3, 2026)

Clean file: `data/keyword-planner-us.csv` (raw: `data/keyword-planner-us-raw-2026-10-03.csv`). Volumes are ranges because the Google Ads account has no ad spend.

| Monthly US searches | Phrases |
|---|---|
| **10K–100K** | towing capacity, tacoma, f150, ram 1500 towing capacity, weight distribution hitch |
| **1K–10K** | 2024 tacoma, 2025 tacoma, tundra, 4runner, silverado 1500, colorado, maverick, tahoe, rav4, highlander towing capacity; towing capacity calculator; towing capacity by vin; payload vs towing capacity; gcwr vs gvwr; best suv for towing |
| **100–1K** | wrangler, grand cherokee, ranger, frontier, ridgeline, telluride, pilot, explorer, silverado (no "1500") towing capacity; towing capacity chart; how much can my car/truck tow; towing laws by state; trailer brake requirements by state |
| **10–100** | trailer speed limits by state |
| **Under 10** | tow rating vin lookup, can i tow a camper with my suv |

- **Truck model pages are the traffic.** Each popular truck gets thousands to tens of thousands of searches a month, before counting year and trim variants.
- **State law pages are small.** "Trailer speed limits by state" gets only 10–100 searches a month, so that's not where the traffic is.
- **Explainer topics are real:** "payload vs towing capacity" and "gcwr vs gvwr" each get 1K–10K.
- **Clicks are cheap:** the estimated average across all phrases is about $0.67 a click, with 73% of searches on phones. Top-of-page bids for model pages are mostly $0.20–$5. The money comes from volume, not from valuable visitors.
- **Rough ad math:** auto and RV display ads pay roughly $15–$30 per 1,000 pageviews. So $3,000 a month takes about 100K–200K pageviews a month, plus affiliate sales on top.

## Verdict (Oct 3, 2026)

**Updated after Similarweb:** less negative than the Ahrefs-only view. A well-sourced site can grow with no links (TowingIQ), and long, specific searches are where the traffic comes from. But visits are short and worth little each, so it takes a lot of pages and a lot of time to earn real money. Still a later project, but worth a small test (about 20 Tacoma pages plus a calculator) once the Astro setup exists.

**Original verdict, from the Ahrefs numbers:**

**Not a strong opportunity as a pure towing database.** Searches are huge, keywords look easy, and links aren't the barrier, but the sites built for this are getting very little traffic, and the oldest one has almost none. The likely reasons are Google favoring dealers and forums, AI answers, and Google penalizing mass-produced spec pages.

What could still work, as a later test, not now:
- A **tool** nobody does well: towing capacity by VIN, or "can my vehicle tow this trailer" with payload math.
- **Real-world content** a database can't fake: weighed rigs, owner photos, videos.
- Folded into a broader **RV / trailer** site where affiliate money is higher.

Recommendation: park it. Keep GeorgiaStormRoof as the main project.

## Are the competitors any good? (checked Oct 3, 2026)

Test: page counts from each sitemap, and each site's 2024 Toyota Tacoma page compared with Toyota's figures (2024: up to 6,500 lb; SR base 3,500 lb; 2016–2023 V6 with tow package: 6,800 lb).

| Site | Pages | Quality | Verdict |
|------|-------|---------|---------|
| towingiq.com | 512 | Every row copied from the Toyota owner manual: model code, GCWR, trailer rating, **with manual page numbers**. Also lists hitch, brake and sway rules from the manual. 2024 Tacoma range 3,500–6,500 is right. | **The real competitor.** Accurate and deep, but only ~512 pages so far. |
| towratings.net | 19,534 | Year-by-year tables, 31 years of Tacoma. Shows **6,500 lb for 2019–2023, but those years were 6,800 lb**. | Big and old, but has errors. |
| autospecdata.com | 4,590 | General spec site (MPG, engine, size) built from EPA and NHTSA data. Towing is one small section. FAQs are template-made: the 2026 towing answer gives 2023's 6,800 lb V6 number, and it asks whether a pickup is front-wheel drive. | Wide but shallow on towing. |
| towcapacity411.com | 4,962 | **Fake data.** The 2024 Tacoma page lists Ford and Ram trims (Lariat, King Ranch, Longhorn), a 6.4L HEMI, max 6,300 lb, and "GVWR 0 lbs." Wrapped in Amazon hitch affiliate links. | Junk. Likely to get wiped out by a Google update. |
| trimfacts.com | 136 | Clean "every configuration, not just the max" layout, calculator, by-VIN page. Covers ~35 popular trucks and SUVs. | Small and new, decent approach. |
| rigcalc.com | 353 | Calculator plus guides. Correctly explains that real capacity is the lowest of tow rating, GCWR math, and payload. Has a corrections log and a named author. | Good quality, calculator-focused, not a database. |
| mintrv.com | 130,362 | Mostly RV listing pages; matches vehicles to RVs. | Different angle (RV shopping). |
| towingservicehub.com | 8,254 | Directory of **tow truck companies**, not towing capacity. Ranks for state speed-limit guides. | Not a direct competitor. |

**Takeaways**
- Quality is mixed. One site is excellent (TowingIQ), two are good but small (RigCalc, TrimFacts), and the rest are big but sloppy or fake.
- The bar to beat is **TowingIQ's level**: owner-manual sources, every configuration, page citations. It's doable, but it's real work per model year, not a weekend AI job.
- A site that's both accurate and complete (TowingIQ's accuracy, TowRatings' coverage, RigCalc's calculator) doesn't exist yet. That's the gap.
- Traffic is still unknown. Good pages don't prove visitors. The Ahrefs/Similarweb check is next.

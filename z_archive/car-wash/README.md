# Car Wash (Winder / Dacula, GA)

## Goal
I have a contact who owns two self-service car washes. What is the viability of creating a directory site for a car wash near Dacula? I think it is in Winder.

## What I know
- Contact owns **two self-service car washes**. Which towns, what they are called, and what he wants from a site are not known yet.
- Winder is about 10 miles east of Dacula. Both are in the area already studied for `../water-damage-georgia`.
- Winder self-service options found by web search (not confirmed to be his):
  - **Downtown Car Wash - Self Serve Winder**, 78 W Athens St. Open 24 hours, 20+ years, self-serve bays plus touch-free automatic. Google-style rating 3.5 from 138 ratings.
  - **24/7 Car Wash**, 93 McNeal Rd. Renovated self-service bays plus touchless. No reviews listed.
  - Competing: Swifty Car Wash (4.5 from 1,147 ratings), WOW Express, Sunshine Car Wash, Athens Street Detail Shop, Wheelin' Deals.
- Near Dacula: Super Car Wash and Emissions (Buford; 24/7 self-serve bays and a touchless drive-thru), Dacula Car Wash (2347 Winder Hwy, automatic plus pressure bays), SuperShine Xpress, Whitecap, Mill Creek.

1. Where are the two car washes (towns, addresses)? Names? Do they have Google Business Profiles with reviews?
2. What do they have: self-serve bays, touchless automatic, vacuums, payment types (cards, Apple Pay, quarters)?
3. What is a typical month's revenue per site? Are they busy, or is he trying to get more customers?
4. What does he want: more drive-in customers, an informational site, or something else (selling the washes, buying more)?
5. Would he pay for anything (listing, ads, a site), and how much?

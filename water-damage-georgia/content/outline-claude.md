Here's the section outline each city page should follow, based on what we built for Lawrenceville:

Headline (H1): "Water Damage Restoration in [City], Georgia"
Call-to-action up top: phone number and form button visible without scrolling, since emergency visitors skim and click
Intro paragraph: names the city, Gwinnett County (or the right county), and two or three real neighborhoods
Local causes section: verified local facts only, like nearby creeks, documented flood risk, and the age of the housing in specific neighborhoods
Services section: extraction, drying, mold remediation, restoration, and the common triggers (burst pipes, storm flooding, sewage backups, slow leaks)
Service area and response time: neighborhoods covered, with a response claim you can actually back up
FAQ section: insurance coverage in Georgia, how fast to respond, and emergency availability in that city
Closing call-to-action: phone number and form again, so it appears more than once on the page

Each page runs roughly 600 to 1,000 words. The sections that make a page distinct from your other cities are 3, 4, and 6, so those need fresh, verified details every time. Sections 2, 5, 7, and 8 can stay similar from city to city.

# {City} — research

Paste this block at the top of a new city file. Check items only when a source is next to the fact.

## Checklist

**Basics**
- [ ] County, ZIP codes, and major roads or highways
- [ ] 4-6 recognizable landmarks (shopping centers, parks, schools, main corridors)
- [ ] Any large named subdivision that might deserve its own page (check competitors for a dedicated page first)

**Neighborhoods and homes**
- [ ] Neighborhood names, with build era for each
- [ ] Foundation types (basement, crawlspace, slab) if you can find them
- [ ] Plumbing-era notes, such as water heater and ice-maker failure windows, or polybutylene for late 1970s to mid-1990s homes. Verify before using.

**Water and weather (verified only)**
- [ ] Nearest creeks and rivers, with a source
- [ ] Documented flood events, with date and source (USGS, NOAA, county, or news)
- [ ] Documented hard freezes and storm events that hit that city
- [ ] Water utility name, plus any documented main breaks or boil advisories
- [ ] Rainfall and humidity averages from NOAA

**Competitor scan**
- [ ] Top 3-4 ranking restoration pages for "[city] water damage"
- [ ] Neighborhoods they name
- [ ] FAQ questions they answer
- [ ] What none of them mention (your opening)

**Logistics**
- [ ] Realistic drive times from where a renter would dispatch

**Source discipline**
- [ ] A link or source name next to every fact, so you can re-verify later

## Keep off the public pages

- **Unverified specifics.** If you can't point to a source, soften it or cut it. "Low-lying" and "older homes" claims need backing.
- **Specific response times** unless the renter will actually hit them.
- **Individual insurance agents or offices.** They're not useful to a visitor in an emergency.
- **Sewage spill logs tied to addresses.** They read as alarmist and carry accuracy risk.
- **School clusters, demographics, and crime.** Great research, wrong page.
- **Health claims about mold.** Stick to "can lead to mold growth," never "will make your family sick."
- **Coverage promises.** Keep the "check with your insurer" language.
- **Flood-zone or FEMA status for specific homes.** Describe regional risk instead.
- **Competitor names.**
- **Certifications, licenses, or "years in business"** that belong to whoever eventually rents the site.
- **Reused paragraphs across cities.** If a paragraph could drop into another city word for word, rewrite it.

**Voice:** drafts that say "we" and "our local team" only work if the renter actually runs the jobs. A request / referral site should not pretend to be the crew.

## Plumbing era (verify on the house)

- Metro Atlanta polybutylene install window is commonly given as **late 1970s–mid-1990s** (often 1978–1995). Gray flexible pipe, sometimes stamped PB2110 / Qest. Do not say a named subdivision "has polybutylene" unless a listing or inspector note says so. [Plumbing Express](https://plumbingexpress.com/home-repiping/polybutylene-replacement/)
- Water heaters and ice-maker / washer hoses commonly fail in the **8–15 year** window. 1995–2005 houses are in that range now. Treat as a research note, not a diagnosis.

**Appliance and supply-line failures.** Hamilton Mill and Apalachee Farms were built mostly between the mid-1990s and the mid-2000s. Water heaters, braided washing-machine hoses, ice-maker lines, and under-sink supply lines installed in that era (or replaced since) are reaching the age where failures become common. These leaks are usually sudden, and they often happen while no one is home.

**Basements and finished lower levels.** Dacula homes sit on different kinds of foundations. Some are on a concrete slab. Some have a crawl space. Others have a basement, and many of those are finished. You will find them in ranch homes at Apalachee Crossing and in parts of Hamilton Mill. ## What Happens When You Call

1. **Call or send the form.** Tell them your address, what kind of water it is, and which rooms are affected.
2. **Talk it through.** The restoration company will ask what happened, where the water went, and whether you have insurance.
3. **Inspection.** A good crew checks for hidden moisture in walls, flooring, and under cabinets, not just what you can see.
4. **Water removal and drying.** Pumps and extractors take out standing water. Air movers and dehumidifiers then run for days, and the crew should show you moisture readings, not just say it looks dry.
5. **Cleanup and repairs.** Depending on the damage, this can include removing wet pad, drywall, or flooring.

You're never obligated to hire anyone, and you're free to get a second opinion before any work starts.

## Quick Steps While You Wait

- If it's safe, shut off the water at the source.
- Stay out of rooms with wet outlets, a sagging ceiling, or sewage.
- Photograph the damage before you move or throw anything away.
- Call your insurance carrier when you can.
- Don't start cutting drywall if you plan to file a claim.

## Insurance and Water Damage in Dacula

Many homeowners policies cover sudden events like a burst pipe or an appliance failure. Slow leaks that went unaddressed, flooding from a creek or street, and sewer backup may not be covered unless you bought those add-ons. Only your policy can answer that, so check with your carrier. A restoration company that works with insurance jobs can document the damage and talk with your adjuster.

## Dacula Water Damage FAQ

**How fast will someone respond?**
It depends on the time of day and how many storms are hitting Gwinnett that night. Ask when you call. Emergency water is worth a call right away, because drying sooner almost always costs less.

**Can I stay in my house?**
Often yes for a clean-water leak in one room. Stay out if there's sewage, a wet electrical panel, or a ceiling coming down.

**How long does drying take?**
A single-room clean-water leak can take a couple of days of fans and dehumidifiers. Wet pad, hardwood, or wall cavities can take longer.

**How soon can mold start?**
Mold can begin to develop within 24 to 48 hours if wet materials sit, which is why waiting until morning can make the job bigger.

**Do I have to hire the company I speak with?**
No. Ask questions, compare, and decide for yourself.

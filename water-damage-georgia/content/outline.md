# City page outline (SEO)

Each city page targets **one phrase**: `water damage restoration in {City}, GA`. Burst pipe, flood, cleanup, extraction, and storm stay on that same URL. Do not make a new page for each synonym.

Google already sees the same job steps, water types, insurance, and FAQ on every city, with only the name swapped. The city page ranks when the **local** block is real and the shared blocks stay short.

If you can paste a section onto another city by changing the name, it is too thin.

## Title and top of the page

- **Title:** Water damage restoration in {City}, GA
- **H1:** same phrase, or close (`Water damage in {City}, GA`)
- **Meta:** one sentence with city, ZIP if it is unique, and the main jobs (burst pipe, leak, storm). Call. Do not explain the middleman.
- **Hero:** county, one local lede, **Call** + request form + first-hour checklist

## Unique to that city (this is the ranking part)

Write this from scratch for each city.

### 1. Water damage in {City}

3–6 sentences. Housing stock, ZIPs, how water actually shows up there.

- Slab vs crawl vs basement
- Townhomes vs 1970s ranches
- Local facts only: lake humidity in Buford, terrace levels in Loganville, upstairs-to-downstairs in Snellville

### 2. Areas people mention

Real streets and pockets, not a county dump. 4–8 names.

Examples: Harbins / Hamilton Mill in Dacula, Collins Hill / Sugarloaf in Lawrenceville.

### 3. What we hear about in {City}

4–6 local causes, not a generic national list. One line each.

This is where these phrases belong if they are true for that city:

- burst pipe {city}
- storm water {city}
- basement flood {city}

### 4. If water is in the house now

Short first-hour list. Name the city once.

- Sewage / sagging ceiling / wet outlets → **call**
- Shut-off drip → `/small-leaks`

### 5. City FAQ

3–5 questions that only make sense there.

Examples:

- Can I stay in a Hamilton Mill slab home after a supply-line leak?
- Does a crawl space under a Suwanee house need to be opened the same day?
- Do you cover Barrow if I am in Winder?

Shared questions (drying time, insurance, small drip) live on `/faq` and `/insurance`. Do not copy them onto every city page.

## Shared, keep short

Use these once, lightly, so the page is complete. Do not rewrite a 400-word essay on every city.

- What a job looks like (inspect → extract → dry → rebuild)
- Clean / gray / sewage
- Insurance: sudden vs slow, photos, do not demo first → link `/insurance`
- Form: name, phone, city, ZIP, what happened
- Other nearby cities (internal links)

## Phrases to use on the page (not new URLs)

Weave into the unique sections, naturally:

- water damage restoration {city}
- water damage restoration {city} ga
- water cleanup {city}
- water extraction {city}
- burst pipe {city}
- flood damage {city}
- storm water damage {city}
- basement flood {city} (only if that city has basements)
- sewage cleanup {city} (only if the shop that takes the call does it)

## What not to put on a city page

- CallRail, forwarding steps, “we are a request page”
- 60-minute truck, IICRC, a license you do not have
- A keyword list at the bottom
- A new URL for `near me`, `company`, or `services`
- Atlanta-level promises on a Gwinnett page, or Gwinnett promises on Winder / Atlanta
- “Our local team” / “our Lawrenceville water damage team” unless that is actually true

## Two pages that should not copy the city template

- **`/gwinnett-county`:** hub. Short county overview + links to the real city pages. Do not reprint Dacula’s streets here.
- **`/atlanta`:** people search Atlanta; we cover Gwinnett and nearby. ZIP on the form. No Buckhead 60-minute claim.

## Test

A Dacula page should read like someone who knows 30019. A Lawrenceville page should not be that Dacula page with the name swapped.

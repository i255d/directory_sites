# Outline vs Claude outline

`outline.md` is a ranking brief for a request site. `outline-claude.md` is a sales-page template from a Lawrenceville draft that sounds like the crew.

## Voice and who we are

| | `outline.md` | `outline-claude.md` |
|---|---|---|
| Site type | Request / help page. Call. Do not explain the middleman. | Sounds like we *are* the Lawrenceville team. |
| Claims | No 60-minute truck, IICRC, or a license we do not have. No “our local team” unless it is true. | Service-area and **response-time** section; claim only what you can back up. Still assumes we dispatch a crew. |
| Services | Does not sell mold / full restoration as “ours.” | Dedicated services block: extraction, drying, mold remediation, restoration. |

Claude’s Lawrenceville drafts in `starting-note.md` match this: “Our local team,” “Our Lawrenceville water damage team,” “we aim to have a crew on the way.” `outline.md` treats that as a lie unless a shop is actually on the brand.

## What each page is for

- **`outline.md`:** one URL, one phrase (`water damage restoration in {City}, GA`). Synonyms stay on that page. Thin city-name swaps lose.
- **`outline-claude.md`:** same H1 idea, but no rule against extra URLs (`near me`, `company`, `services`). No phrase list. Target is implied, not locked.

## Section map

Claude numbers: 1 H1, 2 CTA, 3 intro, 4 local causes, 5 services, 6 service area / response, 7 FAQ, 8 closing CTA.

| Job | `outline.md` | `outline-claude.md` |
|---|---|---|
| H1 / title | Title + H1 + meta. County in the hero. | H1 only. No title/meta. |
| Call / form | Hero + form at the bottom. First-hour checklist in the hero. | CTA at top **and** bottom. Form is a button, not a field list. No first-hour link. |
| Local intro | 3–6 sentences: housing stock, ZIPs, slab/crawl/basement. | City + county + 2–3 neighborhoods. Shorter. |
| Places | Own section. 4–8 **streets and pockets**, not a county dump. | Neighborhoods in the intro **and** again in service area. |
| Causes | 4–6 local jobs. Keywords go here if they are true. | Creeks, documented flood risk, housing age. More “research a flood fact” than “what we hear.” |
| First hour | Own section. Sewage → call. Drip → `/small-leaks`. | Not a section. Speed lives in FAQ + response claim. |
| Job / water types | Shared, short: inspect→dry→rebuild; clean/gray/sewage. | Folded into “services we offer.” |
| Insurance | Shared, short. Link `/insurance`. | One FAQ: does Georgia homeowners cover water. |
| FAQ | 3–5 questions **only that city can ask**. Shared FAQs stay on `/faq`. | Three sitewide FAQs, city name dropped in: insurance, how fast to act, emergency in {city}. |
| Internal links | `/small-leaks`, `/insurance`, `/faq`, other cities. | None. |
| Length | Unique block must be real. Shared blocks stay short. No word count. | 600–1,000 words. Says 3, 4, 6 must be unique; 2, 5, 7, 8 can repeat. |

Claude’s “unique” sections are intro, causes, and service area. `outline.md` also treats first-hour and city FAQ as unique, and treats services / insurance / generic FAQ as the duplicate risk.

## Hub pages

- **`outline.md`:** `/gwinnett-county` is a hub (do not reprint Dacula streets). `/atlanta` is a search-catch page, not a Buckhead promise.
- **`outline-claude.md`:** every city follows the Lawrenceville template. No hub exception.

## Keywords

- **`outline.md`:** weave restoration / cleanup / extraction / burst pipe / flood / storm / basement / sewage on the **same** page. Sewage and basement only if true.
- **`outline-claude.md`:** no keyword list. Relies on H1 + services + city name.

## What `outline.md` forbids that Claude allows or skips

- CallRail / forwarding / “request page” copy (Claude never mentions it)
- Keyword dump at the bottom
- New URL per synonym
- Cross-city promises (Atlanta copy on Gwinnett, Gwinnett copy on Winder)
- Fake “our team” language (Claude’s default)

## Test each one uses

- **`outline.md`:** a Dacula page should read like 30019. Lawrenceville cannot be Dacula with the name swapped.
- **`outline-claude.md`:** 600–1,000 words; sections 3, 4, 6 have fresh verified details.

## Practical take

Use **`outline.md`** for what to write and what not to claim.

Steal from Claude only the **tactics**: phone and form above the fold and again at the bottom; verified local facts (creek, flood note, housing age) inside the unique block.

Do not take Claude’s services section, response-time section, or sitewide FAQ as the city-page pattern. That is how every city ends up the same Lawrenceville page.

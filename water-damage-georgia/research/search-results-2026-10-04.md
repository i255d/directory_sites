# Google results, Oct 4, 2026

Search landed on paid ads plus one broken local site and a national FAQ. Checked whether each is a real crew or a lead mill.

## How to tell

| Signal | Real company | Lead-gen / ad mill |
|---|---|---|
| Address | Shop, warehouse, or named owner home office | UPS Store / “Suite 173” mailbox / no address |
| Phone | Local 404 / 770 / 678 | 800 / 866 / number changes by city |
| Named people | Owner, license, reviews that name a tech | “our network of certified pros” |
| Footer | License, insurance, privacy | “pooled advertisement,” “independent providers,” “we do not guarantee work” |
| SOS / license | Georgia LLC + contractor license | None, or an out-of-state marketing company |
| Same page, many cities | One service area | Template swapped for every metro |
| `gclid` / `gad_source` in the URL | They paid Google for that click | Same — ads prove spend, not a crew |

Georgia contractor license lookup: [sos.ga.gov](https://sos.ga.gov). SOS business search: [ecorp.sos.ga.gov](https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov). BBB and Google Maps photos of trucks / a building are the fastest gut check.

## Local only

People here identify by **Parkview**, not Lilburn. The local fight is the Battle of Five Forks Trickum: Parkview vs Brookwood, six miles apart on the same road. Parkview was named for a view of Stone Mountain that trees and houses have since blocked.

Killian Hill and 29 are the daily roads. Rivermist and the Yellow River houses are older (late 70s–80s), often septic, often a full basement. That is a different Lilburn from a 2020s infill street.

**Public-page opening:** older river / basement stock vs new infill; Five Forks as a road, not a school essay. [Battle of Five Forks Trickum](https://www.gpb.org/blogs/gpb-sports-blog/2017/10/18/battle-of-five-forks-trickum-still-one-of-states-best-rivalries) · [GCPS Parkview](https://www.gcpsk12.org/schools/school-profiles-and-lspi/school-history/high-schools/parkview-high-school)

## Neighborhoods and homes

Oldest housing of this set. City median **~1982**. Biggest lots (~0.44 acre).

| Place | Built | Style / foundation | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downtown Lilburn | Mixed older | Small-town; crawl common | Locals |
| Rivermist | Late 1970s–80s (listings 1975–1986) | 2-story, **full basements**, Yellow River, often septic | Families who want land |
| Rivercliff | ~1977–1999 | Same river / basement / septic pattern | Same |
| Mountain Park | 1965+ | 1970s–80s SFH, bigger lots | Established; Five Forks Trickum |
| Killian Hill | 1970s–90s | Ranch / split / 2-story | Daily-road Lilburn |
| Cherokee Woods / Hanarry | Established | SFH | Older-stock family buyer |
| New infill (Annsbury Park etc.) | 2020s | Smaller lots, craftsman; more slab | First-time, not the river houses |

Not 55+. Empty nesters stay because the lot is big and paid for.

**Plumbing (verify):** This is the city where **polybutylene** is most plausible. Median ~1982 is inside the late-1970s–mid-1990s window. Say homes from that era *may* have gray PB2110 / Qest — verify under sinks / at the heater. Do not write “Rivermist has polybutylene.” New infill does not. Water heaters in 1970s–80s stock are on a later replacement cycle; original supply lines and hose bibs still fail. [Plumbing Express](https://plumbingexpress.com/home-repiping/polybutylene-replacement/)

## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 932–955 ft — lower than Lawrenceville / Dacula. Yellow River corridor ~830–994 ft. [topographic-map.com](https://en-us.topographic-map.com/place-3z64s/Gwinnett-County/)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Yellow River (Rivermist / Rivercliff). Sweetwater Creek at Club Drive (merges with the Yellow; floods Northwood Country Club golf). Jackson Creek (Hillcrest Rd). Camp Creek / Lilburn Greenway. Yellow River WRF is in Lilburn.
- **Floods:** Helene — Hillcrest Rd NW over Jackson Creek closed; Lilburn Greenway Trail closed. Sweetwater Creek at Club Dr 11.1 ft then 12.3 ft (flood warning). Yellow River over flood stage at GA 124. [NWS Helene](https://www.weather.gov/media/ffc/TropicalEventSummary/PSHFFC_2024AL09_Helene_ImpactNarratives.pdf) · [Jackson Progress-Argus](https://www.jacksonprogress-argus.com/local/alcovy-and-yellow-rivers-several-creeks-are-reportedly-flooding-in-gwinnett/article_7dcd1b13-0032-5ef7-93c8-418d046f823b.html)
- **Hard freezes / storms:** Dec 2022 / Jan 2025 county freezes. Later wind — tree on a building, 1700 block Hunters Trace SW. 2018 — trees on lines near Mountain Park. Helene here was mostly flood closures, not a published roof-peel list. [North Gwinnett Voice](https://northgwinnettvoice.com/gwinnett-firefighters-respond-to-increase-in-storm-related-calls/)
- **Utility:** Gwinnett DWR. River lots often still septic. AJC “sea of septic” near Five Forks Trickum / Lake Lucerne is regional color — do not pin it to an address. [DWR](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/water)
- **Main break / boil:** No Lilburn-named boil found in this pass.
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in**. Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%**. [NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016)

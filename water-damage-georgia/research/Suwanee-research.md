- **Landmarks:** Town Center Park (fountain, amphitheater, City Hall); Old Town red caboose / Main Street; George Pierce Park; Suwanee Creek Greenway; Suwanee Creek Park / Martin Farm Park.

## Local only

The railroad tracks split two Suwanees. Town Center Park is one side. Old Town is the other. Freight trains are part of the noise.

Suwanee Creek Greenway floods on a regular gauge: George Pierce, Martin Farm, and the playground behind Suwanee Elementary go under. Many Old Town houses are still on septic; the city says check the Gwinnett water bill — zero sewer charge means septic.

**Public-page opening:** tracks split Town Center from Old Town; the greenway floods on a named gauge. [Town Center vs Old Town](https://atlantakoreatown.com/blogs/cross-the-tracks-suwanees-hidden-other-side) · [Suwanee stormwater / septic note](https://www.suwanee.com/services/public-works-services/stormwater-management)

## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 1,004 ft. [elevationmap.net](https://elevationmap.net/gwinnett-us-1012596109)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Suwanee Creek (USGS 02334885; flood stage **8 ft**). Brushy Creek. Town Center fountain. Chattahoochee / River Club on the west edge. USGS inundation maps Ivy Creek to Noblin Ridge. [USGS Suwanee Creek maps](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3226/)
- **Floods:** Helene — Suwanee Creek Park, Martin Farm, George Pierce, the greenway, and the playground behind Suwanee Elementary flooded. NWS: at 11 ft the greenway has 1–3 ft of water; yards on Bend Creek Trail and Mill Creek Run take water. [NWS Helene](https://www.weather.gov/media/ffc/TropicalEventSummary/PSHFFC_2024AL09_Helene_ImpactNarratives.pdf) · [Jackson Progress-Argus](https://www.jacksonprogress-argus.com/local/alcovy-and-yellow-rivers-several-creeks-are-reportedly-flooding-in-gwinnett/article_7dcd1b13-0032-5ef7-93c8-418d046f823b.html)
- **Hard freezes / storms:** Dec 2022 / Jan 2025 county freezes. 16 Jan 2022 — tree at Moore Rd / Crofton Landing. Later lists: Doe Court / Taylor Rd; Wyndam Hill Dr. [Gwinnett Fire 16 Jan 2022](https://news.monroelocal.org/gwinnett-fire-and-emergency-services-kept-busy-with-trees-and-power-lines-down/)
- **Utility:** Gwinnett DWR for most city water/sewer. Old Town septic = zero sewer charge on the Gwinnett bill. [Suwanee](https://www.suwanee.com/services/public-works-services/stormwater-management) · [DWR](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/water)
- **Main break / boil:** No Suwanee-named boil found in this pass.
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in**. Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%**. [NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016)
- **Industrial (research):** Swift Atlanta / Sharon Industrial Way takes water at high Suwanee Creek stages (NWS categories). Not a chemical accident.

**Sewage logs (keep off public pages):** Nearby Buford spills have hit Suwanee Creek tribs (Woodward Mill Rd, Apr 2024). [EPD 2024](https://wwals.net/pictures/2018-10-31--ga-epd-spill-resolution/spills/2024-04-17--GA-EPD-Sewage-Spills-Report.html)

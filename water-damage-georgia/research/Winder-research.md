## Local only

Winder is Barrow County. Locals get tired of the Gwinnett assumption. The old name was **Jug Tavern**. The courthouse square is the county seat; Fort Yargo is the backyard lake. You can walk there from downtown on the Wilkins Greenway.

City lots are often on Winder sewer (Cedar Creek plant). Outside town it is wells and septic. Coverage from a Gwinnett shop is not automatic.

**Public-page opening:** Barrow / Jug Tavern / Fort Yargo — not a Gwinnett suburb paragraph. [Preserve America / Jug Tavern](https://www.achp.gov/preserve-america/community/winder-georgia) · [Fort Yargo / Wilkins Greenway](https://www.cityofwinder.com/about-us/fort-yargo-state-park)

## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** downtown ~960–980 ft. Confirm a parcel on FEMA’s map; do not copy a Gwinnett number onto a public page.
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Fort Yargo lake. Cedar Creek (city WPCP at 431 Miles Patrick Rd). Beech Creek. Wilkins Greenway follows the creek bottom to the park.
- **Floods / storms:** Helene — Barrow ~6–7 in rain. NWS listed trees and lines (including Winder Hwy in the Walton/Loganville write-up). No Winder river-flood narrative like Suwanee or Lilburn. Treat roofs here as wind/tree, not a Helene flood story. [NWS Helene](https://www.weather.gov/media/ffc/TropicalEventSummary/PSHFFC_2024AL09_Helene_ImpactNarratives.pdf)
- **Hard freezes:** Same Dec 2022 / Jan 2025 arctic pattern as metro Atlanta. Do not invent a Winder-only burst-pipe news hit if you cannot find one — use the regional freeze and Gwinnett/Walton reporting as context only.
- **Utility:** **City of Winder Water Department**. Not Gwinnett DWR. [Winder Water](https://www.cityofwinder.com/government/utilities/water-department)
- **Main break / boil:** No Winder-named boil found in this pass. Do not reuse Gwinnett DWR advisories.
- **Rain / humidity:** GeoStat cites **WINDER 4S, GA US** 1991–2020 normals at about **51 in** (used as a Lawrenceville proxy in some write-ups — prefer this station for Winder). Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%** until a Winder humidity normal is pulled from NCEI.

## Basics

- **County:** City sits on the **Gwinnett–Walton** line. Same mailbox, different county rules and schools.
- **ZIP:** **30052**. [Wikipedia / city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loganville,_Georgia)
- **Roads:** **US 78** (Atlanta Highway in town), **SR 20**, **SR 81**, Rosebud Rd.
- **Landmarks:** 78 / 81 crossing; Judah’s Crossing (Athens Hwy); downtown / Atlanta Highway strip; Rosebud (Gwinnett side); Bay Creek area.
- **Own-page candidates:** No dedicated competitor restoration URL for Rosebud, Bay Creek, or Youth. Keep one Loganville page. Rosebud is a pocket, not a second city.

## Local only

The house’s **county**, not the mailbox, decides the school: Loganville High / Walnut Grove (Walton) vs Grayson or Archer (Gwinnett). “Loganville address” is not a school answer.

In town, US 78 is Atlanta Highway. Rosebud and the Grayson border are the Gwinnett side. Terrace-level / basement water shows up more here than on a slab-only Hamilton Mill street.

**Public-page opening:** one ZIP, two counties; terrace-level water. One sentence on schools is enough — do not write a cluster map. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loganville,_Georgia)

## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 988 ft. [elevationmap.net](https://elevationmap.net/gwinnett-us-1012596109)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Big Flat Creek (city WPCP). Little Haynes Creek. Bay Creek. Alcovy to the north. Gwinnett side uses the county floodplain map (~24,000 acres countywide; new houses 3 ft above BFE). Describe regional creeks, not a house panel.
- **Floods / storms:** Helene — power lines down Loganville Hwy and Creekwood Dr; tree down Eddie Byrd Ln at Winder Hwy. Walton side: 6–7 in rain, trees, one tree on a house (100 block Zion Wood Rd in the Walton narrative). [PerilIQ](https://periliq.com/storm-history/state/georgia/lawrenceville/) · [NWS Helene](https://www.weather.gov/media/ffc/TropicalEventSummary/PSHFFC_2024AL09_Helene_ImpactNarratives.pdf)
- **Hard freezes:** Dec 2022 Gwinnett burst-pipe surge; Walton/Gwinnett cold-snap coverage also reports pipe bursts. Jan 2025 arctic air. [11Alive](https://www.11alive.com/article/news/local/gwinnett-county-911-burst-pipes/85-9439a760-6c6d-48e5-9f31-7beb789d5036) · [Between Community News](https://betweencommunitynews.town.news/g/between-ga/n/290251/cold-snap-causes-pipe-bursts-across-walton-and-gwinnett-counties-how-protect)
- **Utility:** **In the city:** City of Loganville Public Utilities / Water Distribution (also buys wholesale). **Unincorporated Gwinnett side:** often Gwinnett DWR. Check the bill. [Loganville water](https://loganville-ga.gov/departments/public_utilities/water_distribution/index.php)
- **Main break / boil:** Feb 2021 manhole overflow into a Big Flat Creek trib — city said drinking water was not hit (not a boil). No Loganville-named boil found in this pass. [Loganville Patch](https://patch.com/georgia/loganville/loganville-sewage-sewer-did-not-impact-drinking-water-officials)
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in**. Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%**. [NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016)
- **Trees / roofs:** 16 Jan 2022 — 2900 Camp Mitchell Rd and Lenora Rd. 29 Aug 2024 — Bay Creek Rd / McCullers Dr. Lightning at 4490 Brookridge Dr (unincorporated): second floor/attic fire, first floor smoke and water. [Gwinnett Fire 16 Jan 2022](https://news.monroelocal.org/gwinnett-fire-and-emergency-services-kept-busy-with-trees-and-power-lines-down/) · [Loganville lightning](https://news.monroelocal.org/gwinnett-and-walton-county-fire-crews-respond-to-slew-of-storm-related-incidents-saturday/)

## Basics

- **County:** Gwinnett.
- **ZIPs:** **30078** (core / Towne Center) and **30039** (south, Norris Lake). [Gwinnett ZIP list](https://www.ciclt.net/sn/clt/capitolimpact/gw_ziplist.aspx?FIPS=13135)
- **Roads:** Locals say **124**, not Scenic Highway. **US 78**, SR 84, Lenora Church Rd. Town started at the **78 / 124** corner.
- **Landmarks:** The Grove at Towne Center; T.W. Briscoe Park (off Lenora Church — the old civic park); Piedmont Eastside / Target; Presidential Markets; 78/124 rock-store corner.
- **Own-page candidates:** Norris Lake / Norris Lake Shores is large and named, but no strong dedicated competitor restoration URL found in this pass. Keep on the Snellville page unless a competitor ranks a Norris Lake URL. Brookwood Manor is a school-zone brand — not a new page.

## Local only

Locals call the main road **124**. Snellville never had a real downtown until **The Grove**. The old civic park is **Briscoe**, not The Grove.

Yellow River near Snellville has USGS flood maps; No Business Creek backs up toward Lenora.

**Public-page opening:** 124 vs Scenic; Grove vs Briscoe; Yellow River / No Business / Big Haynes by name. [The Grove](https://www.snellville.gov/grove-towne-center) · [USGS Yellow River maps](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/sir20195009)

## Neighborhoods and homes

School zone splits the town (research only on the page — do not write a cluster essay). Only The Grove is a different product (apartments / new downtown).

| Place | Built | Style / foundation | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Grove / Towne Center | 2020s | Mixed-use, apartments (The Tomlin) | Renters; not the ranch buyer |
| Briscoe Park area | 1980s–90s | Split-level / ranch / 2-story; crawl and basement both show up | Long-time families |
| Brookwood Manor | Established | SFH, swim/tennis | Brookwood school families |
| Brookwood Village | ~2002 | Smaller / some attached | Starter in the Brookwood name |
| Lenora Springs / Lenora | 1980s–90s | Mixed; some no HOA | South Gwinnett, larger lots |
| Oak Grove Glen | Newer | New SFH | First-time / move-up |
| Havenstone | **2003–2006** | Gated, brick-4-side | Move-up Brookwood families |
| Norris Lake / Shores | **~1980–1989** | Cottage / ranch / lake | Lake people, 30039 |
| Plantation Forest | ~1989 | SFH | Established south Snellville |

**Foundations:** 1980s ranches / splits often crawl; lake and hillside lots more basement. New Grove product is slab / podium mixed-use. Confirm on the house.

**Plumbing (verify):** Briscoe, Lenora, Norris Lake, Plantation Forest sit in the **polybutylene window** (late 1970s–mid-1990s). Say “homes from that era may have it — look for gray PB2110,” not “Norris Lake has polybutylene.” Havenstone / Grove are after that window (heater / hose age for 2003–06). ## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 984 ft — lower than Lawrenceville / Buford. [topographic-map.com](https://en-us.topographic-map.com/place-3z64s/Gwinnett-County/)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Yellow River (USGS 02206500 near Snellville; inundation maps from River Drive to US 78). No Business Creek (Lenora). Big Haynes Creek at Lenora Road. Jacks Creek. Watson Creek. Briscoe Park 6-acre lake. Norris Lake (30039). Grove greenway follows a mostly dry creek bed. [USGS SIR 2019-5009](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/sir20195009)
- **Floods:** Helene — Big Haynes at Lenora Road over flood stage (10.1 ft). No Business Creek at Lee Road at flood stage (9.1 ft). Yellow River over flood stage at GA 124 (15.3 ft). County put Snellville in the south/west flood belt. [Jackson Progress-Argus](https://www.jacksonprogress-argus.com/local/alcovy-and-yellow-rivers-several-creeks-are-reportedly-flooding-in-gwinnett/article_7dcd1b13-0032-5ef7-93c8-418d046f823b.html)
- **Hard freezes / storms:** Dec 2022 / Jan 2025 county freezes. 16 Jan 2022 — tree limb through a roof (Boone St area); trees/wires on Amy Rd, Shiloh/Ross, Mink Livsey, Spain/Johnson, Everson Rd. 2018 — trees on Five Forks Trickum between Lawrenceville and Snellville. [Gwinnett Fire 16 Jan 2022](https://news.monroelocal.org/gwinnett-fire-and-emergency-services-kept-busy-with-trees-and-power-lines-down/)
- **Utility:** Gwinnett DWR. [DWR](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/water)
- **Main break / boil:** **31 Mar 2020** — main break, boil advisory for the **Norris Lake** area; rescinded 1 Apr 2020. That is 30039, not Towne Center. [AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/local/gwinnett-issues-boil-water-advisory-due-water-main-break/zF5ZhocIAcIYghYT9ER8UI/) · [OCG News](https://ocgnews.com/gwinnett-county-rescinds-boil-water-advisory/)
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in**. Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%**. [NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016)

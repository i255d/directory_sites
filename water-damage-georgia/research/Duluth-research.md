## Basics

- **County:** Gwinnett.
- **ZIPs:** **30096, 30095**; Sugarloaf Country Club often **30097**. 30026 / 30029 appear as Duluth / North Metro in directories. [Gwinnett ZIP list](https://www.ciclt.net/sn/clt/capitolimpact/gw_ziplist.aspx?FIPS=13135)
- **Roads:** I-85, Pleasant Hill Rd, Peachtree Industrial (**PIB**), SR 120 / Duluth Hwy, Sugarloaf Pkwy.
- **Landmarks:** Town Green / Parsons Alley (the rebuilt downtown); **Gwinnett Place** (Pleasant Hill at I-85 — dead since 2021, still used for directions); PIB; Sugarloaf Country Club / TPC; Cardinal Lake.
- **Own-page candidates:** **Sugarloaf** already has a SERVPRO URL (parkway + mills + country club mashed together). A Sugarloaf CC page only makes sense if you write the gated 30097 product, not Sugarloaf Mills (Lawrenceville). [SERVPRO Sugarloaf](https://www.servpro.com/locations/ga/sugarloaf)

## Local only

Locals still give directions off **Gwinnett Place** even though the mall has been dead since 2021. Downtown is Town Green / Parsons Alley. **PIB** is Peachtree Industrial.

Townhomes and upstairs-unit leaks are a Duluth problem more than a ranch-house problem. Rogers Creek / Chattahoochee side still has pockets of septic the county has been sewing (Pinecrest project, ~500 parcels).

**Public-page opening:** dead mall as a landmark; PIB townhomes; do not treat Sugarloaf CC as “downtown Duluth.” [Gwinnett Place Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwinnett_Place_Mall) · [Downtown Duluth](https://downtownduluthga.net/directory.php)

## Neighborhoods and homes

City median year built **~1992**. Townhomes and the country club are different products.

| Place | Built | Style / foundation | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Town Green / Parsons Alley | Rebuilt 2010s | Apartments, shops, some older houses | Renters, diners |
| Pleasant Hill / Gwinnett Place | 1980s–90s | Condos, townhomes, tired SFH; more slab / crawl | Starter / renter around the dead mall |
| PIB | Mixed | Townhomes, 2-story — **upstairs-unit leaks** | More attached housing than Dacula |
| Sugarloaf Country Club | Late 1990s–2000s | Gated estate; basements common in listings | Executive / high HOA. ZIP 30097 |
| Sugarloaf Park | Smaller, next door | SFH | Same corridor, less club |
| Cardinal Lake Estates | Older, 650+ homes | Modest SFH, lake | Duluth High families |

## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 974 ft. [elevationmap.net](https://elevationmap.net/gwinnett-us-1012596109)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Chattahoochee / Rogers Creek. Cardinal Lake. City floodplain rules and 20/50/75-ft stream buffers; some lots in the 2,000-ft Chattahoochee (MRPA) corridor — describe the corridor, not a parcel. [Duluth flood brochure](https://cms4files.revize.com/duluth/Document%20Center/Services/planning%20and%20development/stormwater/Flooding%20and%20flood%20plain%20Management/brochure.pdf)
- **Floods:** Helene flooded the Chattahoochee basin; county rain 9–10 in. Pleasant Hill / Indian Trail is a known flood pocket (Indian Trail at I-85 photographed in Helene). [Atlanta News First](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2024/09/27/hurricane-helene-took-its-toll-parts-gwinnett-county/)
- **Hard freezes / storms:** Dec 2022 burst pipes (Villas at Duluth). Jan 2025 arctic air. 16 Jan 2022 — tree at Hill Dr / Cardinal Lake Dr. 2018 — tree on a house near Buckingham Place / Castlerock (Berkeley Lake / Duluth edge). [Gwinnett Fire 16 Jan 2022](https://news.monroelocal.org/gwinnett-fire-and-emergency-services-kept-busy-with-trees-and-power-lines-down/)
- **Utility:** Gwinnett DWR in most of the city. Pinecrest project aimed to take ~500 parcels off septic along Rogers Creek. [EPD Pinecrest notice](https://epd.georgia.gov/document/document/bv-067-23-06-gwinnett-county-public-noticepdf/download) · [DWR](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/water)
- **Main break / boil:** DWR listed a **planned** shutoff 9 Sep 2026 at 3525 Mall Blvd (Esplanade at Gwinnett) — scheduled work, not a boil. No Duluth-named boil found in this pass. [DWR outages](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/water/report-problem/outages)
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in**. Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%**. [NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016)

## Basics

- **County:** Gwinnett (city also touches Hall). Rest Haven sits on the Hall–Gwinnett line.
- **ZIPs:** **30518, 30519** (30515 listed in some directories). Sugar Hill shares 30518. [Gwinnett ZIP list](https://www.ciclt.net/sn/clt/capitolimpact/gw_ziplist.aspx?FIPS=13135)
- **Roads:** I-985, I-85, Mall of Georgia Blvd, Friendship Rd, Buford Dam Rd, Main St, Buford Hwy.
- **Landmarks:** **Mall of Georgia** (“the Mall” — nobody here means Gwinnett Place); Phillip Beard Stadium (Wolves); Buford Dam / Lake Lanier; downtown Main Street; Gwinnett tag office on Mall of Georgia Blvd.
- **Own-page candidates:** No single HOA with a dedicated competitor URL like Hamilton Mill. Lake Lanier shoreline is a different *life*, not a subdivision URL. Keep one Buford page.

## Local only

“The Mall” means Mall of Georgia. Buford City Schools is its own system, not GCPS — Friday nights are Wolves football at Phillip Beard Stadium.

Lake people and mall people are different Bufords. Friendship Road / Mall of Georgia is one life. Rest Haven and the Lanier shoreline is another. Waterfront lots are often septic; I-985 / in-town is usually sewer.

**Public-page opening:** Mall of Georgia vs Gwinnett Place; lake lots vs mall-corridor houses. One sentence on City Schools is enough — do not write a cluster essay. ## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 1,115–1,155 ft — one of the higher Gwinnett cities. Lake Lanier full pool is **1,071 ft**. [topographic-map.com](https://en-us.topographic-map.com/place-3z64s/Gwinnett-County/) · [USACE Lanier](https://www.sam.usace.army.mil/Missions/Civil-Works/Recreation/Lake-Sidney-Lanier/Purposes/)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Lake Sidney Lanier / Buford Dam (flood storage, drinking water). Richland Creek. Suwanee Creek headwaters. Ivy Creek greenway at the mall. Describe the shoreline as a regional flood/storage risk — do not assign a FEMA panel to a house.
- **Floods:** Dam releases raise the Chattahoochee downstream. Lanier can rise 14 ft above full pool; record was 1964 (~6 ft above full). Helene flooded Suwanee Creek parks just south of here. [USACE](https://www.sam.usace.army.mil/Missions/Civil-Works/Recreation/Lake-Sidney-Lanier/Purposes/)
- **Hard freezes / storms:** Dec 2022 / Jan 2025 county freezes. 16 Jan 2022 — tree onto a house, 100 block Legion Dr NE; trees/wires on Old Cumming, Crestworth, Wilson, Thompson Mill, N Bogan, Level Creek, I-985, Tuggle, Silk Leaf. [Gwinnett Fire 16 Jan 2022](https://news.monroelocal.org/gwinnett-fire-and-emergency-services-kept-busy-with-trees-and-power-lines-down/)
- **Utility:** **City of Buford Water Department** — source is Lake Lanier, plus some purchase from Gwinnett. Unincorporated / mall-edge parcels may still be Gwinnett DWR — check the bill. [City of Buford Water](https://www.cityofbuford.com/utilities/water-department) · [2024 CCR](https://www.cityofbuford.com/sites/default/files/uploads/water/2024.water-quality.report.pdf)
- **Main break / boil:** No Buford-city boil advisory found in this pass. Do not reuse the Norris Lake or Dacula DWR advisories.
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in**. Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%**. [NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016)
- **Industrial (research):** City pretreatment into Southside (Suwanee Creek) and Westside (Richland Creek) plants — permitted, not an accident. [EPD Buford pretreatment](https://epd.georgia.gov/document/document/city-buford-2021-industrial-pretreatment-program-modification/download)

**Sewage logs (keep off public pages):** 1,395 gal at 654 Riverside Rd into Richland Creek (26 Mar 2024). 6,015 gal at 4185 Woodward Mill Rd into a Suwanee Creek trib (12 Apr 2024). [EPD 2024](https://wwals.net/pictures/2018-10-31--ga-epd-spill-resolution/spills/2024-04-17--GA-EPD-Sewage-Spills-Report.html)

## Basics

- **County:** Gwinnett. Incorporated city is tiny; most “Grayson” houses are unincorporated.
- **ZIP:** **30017**. Grayson High’s **mailing address is Loganville**. [Gwinnett ZIP list](https://www.ciclt.net/sn/clt/capitolimpact/gw_ziplist.aspx?FIPS=13135) · [GCPS Grayson High](https://www.gcpsk12.org/schools/school-profiles-and-lspi/school-history/high-schools/grayson-high-school)
- **Roads:** **SR 20** is Grayson Highway one way and Loganville Highway the other. Rosebud Rd, Pharrs Rd.
- **Landmarks:** Uptown / The Railyard; Kroger on Grayson Hwy at Rosebud; Tribble Mill Park (county park, not a city park); Grayson High (building in Grayson, mail Loganville).
- **Own-page candidates:** Wheatfields / Wheatfields Reserve is the name people mean — no dedicated competitor restoration URL found. Great River at Tribble Mill is already named on Lawrenceville competitor pages. Keep one Grayson page. SERVPRO has a thin `/grayson` city URL.

## Local only

Incorporated Grayson is tiny (~4,700 people). Most people who say they live in Grayson live in unincorporated Gwinnett.

The town was first called **Trip** (“a trip from Lawrenceville”). Alcovy at New Hope Road is the flood gauge locals hear on Helene nights. Tribble Mill is the county park everyone means.

**Public-page opening:** tiny city vs huge “Grayson” mailing; Trip; Hwy 20 changes name; Alcovy at New Hope. [Grayson Comp Plan](https://cityofgrayson.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/2024-GRAYSON-COMP-PLAN-UPDATE-FINAL-02-19-24.pdf)

## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 1,060 ft. [elevationmap.net](https://elevationmap.net/gwinnett-us-1012596109)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Alcovy River at New Hope Road (flood stage **9 ft**). Tribble Mill Park lakes. City plan keeps floodplain / floodway mostly off-limits — still do not assign a panel to a house.
- **Floods:** Helene — minor flooding on the Alcovy at New Hope Rd (10.6 ft, crest ~10.7). At 11 ft NWS says moderate; water at the bottom of the bridge. [FOX 5](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/weather/atlanta-weather-powerful-tropical-cyclone-will-impact-georgia) · [Jackson Progress-Argus](https://www.jacksonprogress-argus.com/local/alcovy-and-yellow-rivers-several-creeks-are-reportedly-flooding-in-gwinnett/article_7dcd1b13-0032-5ef7-93c8-418d046f823b.html)
- **Hard freezes / storms:** Dec 2022 / Jan 2025 county freezes. 16 Jan 2022 — tree at Chandler Rd / Tribble Creek Dr. No Grayson-only Helene roof list found. [Gwinnett Fire 16 Jan 2022](https://news.monroelocal.org/gwinnett-fire-and-emergency-services-kept-busy-with-trees-and-power-lines-down/)
- **Utility:** Gwinnett DWR for most addresses. [DWR](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/water)
- **Main break / boil:** No Grayson-named boil found in this pass.
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in**. Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%**. [NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016)
- **Industrial (research):** Light-industry park converting septic to sewer (2023 city plan). No chemical accident found.

**Sewage logs (keep off public pages):** 8,208 gal from a Grayson sewer line into a Big Haynes Creek trib after prior storm damage (1 Nov 2017). [AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/local/storm-damage-causes-200-gallon-sewage-spill-gwinnett/ficm2tfipqmuxXruqSVSbM/)

## Basics

- **County:** Gwinnett (county seat).
- **ZIPs:** **30042, 30043, 30044, 30045, 30046, 30049**. 30043 / 30044 / 30046 are the ones competitors repeat. [Gwinnett ZIP list](https://www.ciclt.net/sn/clt/capitolimpact/gw_ziplist.aspx?FIPS=13135)
- **Roads:** **316**, **SR 20**, **124**, Sugarloaf Pkwy, Hurricane Shoals Rd, Old Norcross Rd, Langley Dr.
- **Landmarks:** the Square (historic courthouse / Crogan); the Lawn (amphitheater, HYRO); **GJAC** on Langley (the building people actually use for court); Rhodes Jordan Park (“RJ Park”); Sugarloaf Mills; Collins Hill High.
- **Own-page candidates:** Hamilton Mill and Sugarloaf Country Club already have competitor URLs — those are not Lawrenceville pages. Edgewater / Chandler Pond / Knollwood: no dedicated competitor page found. Keep them as named pockets, not new URLs. [SERVPRO Sugarloaf](https://www.servpro.com/locations/ga/sugarloaf)

## Local only

Downtown has two centers locals name separately: **the Square** and **the Lawn**. Court is GJAC on Langley, not the clock-tower courthouse.

During Helene, county officials flagged Wolf Creek (it dumps into the Yellow River) and Pew Creek at Patterson Road. South and west Lawrenceville flood more than the Collins Hill / Sugarloaf side.

**Public-page opening:** Square vs Lawn vs GJAC, plus Wolf Creek / Pew Creek — not a generic “Gwinnett county seat” paragraph. [Lawrenceville Lawn](https://www.lawrencevillega.org/556/Lawrenceville-Lawn) · [Atlanta News First Helene](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2024/09/25/gwinnett-county-preps-flooding-other-impacts-hurricane-helene/)

## Neighborhoods and homes

Downtown is old. Named swim/tennis lakes are late-80s–90s family product. Not 55+.

| Place | Built | Style / foundation | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downtown / Square / Lawn | Historic + new infill | Mix; older = crawl more likely | Renters + walk-to-Lawn |
| Collins Hill | 1990s | Brick 2-story SFH | Collins Hill families |
| Sugarloaf (Mills side) | 1990s–2000s | SFH + some townhomes | Not the same as Sugarloaf CC (Duluth 30097) |
| Edgewater | **1989–2000** | John Wieland, 3-side brick, lake | Collins Hill families |
| Chandler Pond / Knollwood Lakes | 1990s | Swim/tennis, pond/lake | Same family buyer |
| Old Norcross | Older corridor | Mix | Less HOA |
| River Colony | Late 1980s | Mix | Families near the highway |
| Great River at Tribble Mill | 2000s | 525-home swim/tennis | Grayson / south Lawrenceville edge |

**Foundations:** Lake / hillside swim-tennis streets often have basements in listings. Do not call the whole city slab or basement.

**Plumbing (verify):** Edgewater / River Colony / late-80s stock sit in or next to the polybutylene window (late 1970s–mid-1990s). 1990s Collins Hill / Sugarloaf Mills side is water-heater / hose age. Confirm PB on the house. [Plumbing Express](https://plumbingexpress.com/home-repiping/polybutylene-replacement/)

## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 1,024 ft. [topographic-map.com](https://en-us.topographic-map.com/place-3z64s/Gwinnett-County/)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Wolf Creek → Yellow River. Pew Creek (gauge near Lawrenceville). Shoal Creek. Alcovy on the east. Rhodes Jordan fishing lake. Chandler Pond / Knollwood / Edgewater are named pond neighborhoods.
- **Floods:** Helene — county said Wolf Creek has a high flood chance if >6 in rain in 24–48 hours. Pew Creek at Patterson Road was flooding (~11 ft). South and west Lawrenceville, Woodward Crossing, Indian Trail are the usual pockets.

## Local only

Hamilton Mill is not “Dacula.” People who live here say Hamilton Mill or HM. Kids go to Mill Creek High in Hoschton (Hawks), not Dacula High. Mill Creek was built to pull kids off Dacula and North Gwinnett.

The golf course is the spine. Sections have their own names (Highpointe, Apalachee Crossing). A 1990s house here is due for a water heater and roof, not a “new construction” pitch.

**Public-page opening:** Exit 120, unincorporated, Mill Creek — not the Dacula High page. ## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** I-85 ridge, higher than old Dacula. Nearby Buford ~1,115 ft; Dacula ~1,027 ft. Mill Creek / Duncan Creek sit below the parkway. [Gwinnett elevations](https://en-us.topographic-map.com/place-3z64s/Gwinnett-County/)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Duncan Creek (the old mill that named Mill Creek High). Golf-club lakes (Lake Forest / Lakeside). Ivy Creek / Mall of Georgia side to the west. No named river through the HOA.
- **Floods:** Same Gwinnett Helene watch as Dacula. County said south and west flood first (Snellville, south Lawrenceville, Norcross), not this exit. No HM-named USGS flood crest found. [Atlanta News First Helene](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2024/09/25/gwinnett-county-preps-flooding-other-impacts-hurricane-helene/)
- **Hard freezes / storms:** Dec 2022 and Jan 2025 county-wide pipe-freeze events. No HM-named tree-through-roof report found. Closest logged trees: Heatherton Rd (Dacula) and Buford / Mall-of-Georgia wind lists. NWS: most Georgia Helene structure damage was falling trees. [11Alive Dec 2022](https://www.11alive.com/article/news/local/gwinnett-county-911-burst-pipes/85-9439a760-6c6d-48e5-9f31-7beb789d5036) · [NWS Helene narratives](https://www.weather.gov/media/ffc/TropicalEventSummary/PSHFFC_2024AL09_Helene_ImpactNarratives.pdf)
- **Utility:** Gwinnett DWR where sewer/water exists. Dacula rules ban new septic when sewer has capacity. No HM-specific boil advisory found. Jun 2024 Dacula-area advisory was a contractor hit — check the DWR map before attributing it to HM. [DWR](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/water)
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in**. Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield **~68%**. [NCEI US1GAGW0016](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016) · [Atlanta weatherbox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template:Atlanta_weatherbox)

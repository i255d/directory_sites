## Local only

A Dacula mailing address does not mean Dacula High. Old town around Broad Street feeds the Dacula cluster. Hamilton Mill feeds Mill Creek. Harbins Road / New Hope was pulled out of Dacula in 2009 and now feeds Archer. Locals ask which cluster before they ask which street.

The town name is a mash of Decatur and Atlanta. The original settlement was Feemantown at Fence and Dacula roads, then Hoke, then Dacula after the railroad.

**Public-page opening (what competitors skip):** the 30019 mailbox vs the high-school cluster. Do not turn that into a school-zone essay on the city page — one or two sentences is enough. [Dacula Wire school-cluster map](https://daculawire.com/which-schools-does-a-dacula-address-feed-the-four-cluster-map-every-buyer-needs/) · [GCPS Dacula High history](https://www.gcpsk12.org/schools/school-profiles-and-lspi/school-history/high-schools/dacula-high-school)

**Foundations:** Do not write “Dacula is slab.” Listings in HM and Apalachee Crossing show basements; older Broad Street stock is mixed. Confirm on the house.

**Plumbing (verify):** 1995–2005 houses are in the water-heater / supply-hose / ice-maker window. Polybutylene is a **late-1970s–mid-1990s** metro Atlanta product. A 1998-median city is mostly after that window; old town and any 1978–1995 house still needs a look (gray pipe, PB2110 / Qest). Do not name a subdivision as PB without a listing or inspector note. ## Water and weather

- **Elevation:** about 1,027–1,037 ft. [topographic-map.com](https://en-us.topographic-map.com/place-3z64s/Gwinnett-County/) · [elevationmap.net](https://elevationmap.net/gwinnett-us-1012596109)
- **Rivers / lakes / ponds:** Apalachee River (gauge at Fence Road, USGS 02218565, datum 930.2 ft). Alcovy River south of 316. Trophy Club of Apalachee golf ponds. FEMA special flood hazard along the Apalachee; Georgia 50-ft stream buffer. Describe the river corridor, not a house’s FEMA panel. [USGS Apalachee at Fence Road](https://streamflowdata.com/stations/apalachee-river-at-fence-road-near-dacula-ga)
- **Floods:** Sept 2009 — Apalachee at Fence Road peaked 8.56 ft. Helene (Sept 2024) was a Gwinnett-wide flood watch; county flagged south/west more than this ridge. [USGS Sept 2009 peaks](https://ga.water.usgs.gov/www2/flood/flooding-sept09/peak-gageheights.html) · [Atlanta News First Helene](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2024/09/25/gwinnett-county-preps-flooding-other-impacts-hurricane-helene/)
- **Hard freezes / storms:** Same Dec 2022 burst-pipe surge and Jan 2025 arctic air as the rest of Gwinnett. 16 Jan 2022 — tree down, 1900 block Heatherton Rd NE. 2018 — baseball hail (2.50 in) reported near Dacula. [11Alive Dec 2022](https://www.11alive.com/article/news/local/gwinnett-county-911-burst-pipes/85-9439a760-6c6d-48e5-9f31-7beb789d5036) · [Gwinnett Fire 16 Jan 2022](https://news.monroelocal.org/gwinnett-fire-and-emergency-services-kept-busy-with-trees-and-power-lines-down/) · [2018 hail](https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/gwinnett_county_georgia/2018)
- **Utility:** Gwinnett County DWR. [gwinnettcounty.com/water](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/water)
- **Main break / boil:** 14 Jun 2024 — contractor hit a main; DWR issued a Dacula-area boil advisory after pressure dropped. Norris Lake (Snellville 30039) had a separate March 2020 advisory — do not paste that onto Dacula. [r/Gwinnett Dacula BWA](https://www.reddit.com/r/Gwinnett/comments/1dgg5ht/boil_water_advisory_dacula_20240614/)
- **Rain / humidity:** NOAA Lawrenceville 4.2 SW **53.53 in** annual (1991–2020). Humidity: Atlanta Hartsfield proxy **~68%**. [NCEI US1GAGW0016](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataTypes=MLY-PRCP-NORMAL&dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&format=pdf&stations=US1GAGW0016) · [Atlanta weatherbox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template:Atlanta_weatherbox)
- **Industrial (research):** CSX derailment south of 316 and 29 spilled methanol and ethylene glycol; EPD sampled the Alcovy. Use only if you can keep it regional, not alarmist. [The Red & Black](https://www.redandblack.com/news/officials-316-a-hot-zone/article_a55be5a1-e7f6-5ac4-aa0f-380fcfff804f.html)

**Sewage logs (keep off public pages):** 2,503 gal at 2910 Old Fountain Rd (10 Jan 2021) into an Apalachee tributary. 741 gal at 1344 Winder Hwy (24 Aug 2023) into the Alcovy.

## First hour (what they tell the homeowner)

**Water Pro** ([what to do if your house floods](https://www.waterpro-inc.com/what-to-do-if-your-house-floods/))
- Shut the main water if you can reach it safely.
- If water is near outlets or the breaker, stay out. Kill power only from a dry place.
- Kids and pets out of the wet rooms. Treat sewage / outdoor water as dirty.
- Do not run HVAC. Do not use household fans as the dry-out. Do not walk wet carpet more than you must. Do not plug in anything that got wet.
- Stay-or-go: “Depending on safety and moisture… we evaluate electrical or mold hazards before you decide.” Sewage: stay out.

**SERVPRO** (national water FAQ + South sewage page)
- Shut off the source if you can; plumber if you cannot.
- Mop / blot. **Do not use a household vacuum.**
- Foil or paper under furniture legs. Move books and pillows off wet carpet.
- Do not use wet electrics. Consider flipping the wet circuits.
- Sewage: no contact, shut HVAC, people and pets out, then call. They will help you call insurance.

**ServiceMaster Gwinnett** ([large loss blog](https://www.smgwin.com/blog/2025/5/26/what-customers-need-to-know-about-large-water-losses-in-their-homes))
- Cut the water at the source or the main.
- Do not wade toward outlets.
- Kill the breaker only if you can reach it dry.
- Call now. Take your own photos. Keep receipts.

**RestoPros**
- Call right away. They tell you what to do on that call.
- Household fan is not enough (moisture in walls / floors).

**SERVPRO HM** — no extra first-hour list beyond the brand FAQ.

## What this means for our FAQ

- Do not steal “60 minutes.” The shops with a real Gwinnett address mostly say **24/7** and stop. Water Pro is the only one of these five that prints a number, and it is “about 30 minutes from Dacula, traffic permitting,” on a Duluth page, not a Dacula guarantee.
- The clock they advertise is **dispatch / truck**, not “we call you back in 10 minutes.” Form callback is unpublished across the board.
- All five will say they take 30019. Only Water Pro and SERVPRO HM talk like they live next to it.
- Sewage is a yes for Water Pro and both SERVPROs. Do not put sewage cleanup on our page until *our* renter says yes.
- All five will talk to insurance. None print a carrier name we should copy.
- First-hour copy we can use without pretending to be them: shut the water, stay off wet power, photo, stay out of sewage, do not demo, do not household-vac, do not run HVAC on a dirty job.

Ask the renter the questions in `questions-for-them.md` anyway. These pages are what they tell Google, not what they will sign for our number.

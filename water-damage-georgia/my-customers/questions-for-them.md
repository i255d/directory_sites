# Questions for the company that wants the calls

Ask these before we put a response time, a “we,” or a service claim on a city page. Get the time answers in writing, with the clock defined. If they will not put a number on it, the page stays “we cannot promise a time.”

## Response time

- What do you promise on a weekday, in daylight, for old Dacula (Fence / Dacula Road / the plaza)?
- Same question after 10 p.m., and on a Sunday.
- Same question for Hamilton Mill (Exit 120) and for Harbins / New Hope.
- What happens on a Helene-style night when Gwinnett is already flooded — do you still take new jobs, or do you queue them?
- Is the clock “we call you back” or “a truck is in the driveway”? Those are different sentences.
- Where does the truck leave from? (Street or at least the city.)
- If you cannot hit that time, what should the page say instead of a number?

## Who answers

- Do you answer this number live, 24/7, or does it go to voicemail / an answering service?
- How fast do you call back a web form?
- Can the homeowner stay on the line until a crew is assigned?

## Where you will actually go

- Confirm 30019: old town, Apalachee Farms, Harbins, Hamilton Mill.
- Do you take Winder / Barrow, Loganville / Walton, or only Gwinnett?
- Any street or ZIP you will refuse?

## What jobs you take

- Burst pipe, water heater, appliance hose — yes?
- Storm / roof leak / tree on the roof — extract only, or roof tarp too?
- Finished basement and crawl space — yes?
- Sewage / toilet backup — yes? If no, the page cannot say sewage cleanup.
- Category 3 / standing water from the yard or the Apalachee — yes?
- Mold that is already growing, or only wet-building dry-out?
- Rebuild (drywall, flooring) or mitigation only?

## Insurance

- Will you talk to the adjuster and bill the carrier, or is the homeowner paying you and filing on their own?
- Preferred vendor for any carriers we should not name on the page?
- Do you need them to call insurance before you roll?

## What we can put in their mouth

- Can the site say “we” and “our crew,” or must it stay “call and a company will follow up”?
- Legal name, license number, and years in business — only if they want those on the site. Do not invent them.
- Any IICRC or similar claim they can prove, and whose name it is under.

## While the homeowner waits

- What should they do in the first hour that you actually want them to do?
- Anything they should not do (fans, pulling pad, cutting drywall)?
- When can they stay in the house, and when do you tell them to leave?
